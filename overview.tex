\resultentry{001}{Erd\H{o}s--Straus identities and obstructions}{%
\textbf{ES-001} (\texttt{[PROVED]}) proves via \texttt{es\_covering\_217mod264} that every natural $n\equiv217\pmod{264}$ has positive natural denominators $x,y,z$ with $4x=n+3$, $y=n(n+3)$, $11z=n(n+3)$, and $\frac4n=\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z$ (compatible rational-equality lemma \texttt{es\_identity\_217mod264}; example $n=1009$). \texttt{ESIdentityCRT.lean} proves the exact six covered subclasses modulo~$9240$, positive covering witnesses for their members, and exactly one out of eleven subclasses of each parent residue modulo~$840$ (a residue count, not a prime-density or priority theorem). The former novelty claim is withdrawn in light of Lopez (2024), \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10319v4}{arXiv:2206.10319v4}. %
\textbf{ES-002} (\texttt{[PROVED]}) exports \texttt{es\_crt\_escape}: for finitely many progressions with $8\mid A_i$ and an explicit local obstruction for each $B_i$ (an odd prime $q\mid A_i$ with $B_i$ nonsquare mod~$q$, or $B_i\not\equiv1\pmod8$), infinitely many primes in each listed square class modulo~$840$ avoid every progression, proved via CRT and Mathlib's general Dirichlet theorem with no custom axioms. The Schinzel--Mordell implication from integer polynomial identities to these local obstructions remains unformalized.%
}{\href{https://github.com/derekblair/math-proofs/blob/master/preprints/erdos-straus-identity-217mod264-October-3-2026/paper.pdf}{ES-001 PDF (historical)}\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace\href{https://github.com/derekblair/math-proofs/blob/master/preprints/erdos-straus-finite-identity-barrier-October-4-2026/paper.pdf}{ES-002 PDF (historical)}\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace\href{https://github.com/derekblair/math-proofs/blob/master/lean/docs/001.md}{Lean scope}}

\resultentry{002}{Collatz convergent control (gap-factor theory)}{%
Twelve modules (\texttt{CollatzGapCF}, \texttt{C24}, \texttt{C24Lemma3}, \texttt{Sinh}, \texttt{Delta}, \texttt{Convex}, \texttt{C23}, \texttt{C22C25}, \texttt{Factor}, \texttt{C29}, \texttt{C29Mirror}, \texttt{Hump}) prove general continued-fraction identities, analytic estimates, discrete monotonicity, C26 margin duality, repaired \texttt{GapCF.c26c} selector equivalences, conditional odd-block argmin assemblies, interior discrepancy positivity (\texttt{c29\_strict}), and the C64/C64b hump-law identity kernel (\texttt{[PROVED]} with standard axioms only). \texttt{CollatzGapFactor.lean} defines $G(t)=\frac{Q(t+1)}{Q(t)}\frac{2^{x(t)}-1}{2^{x(t+1)}-1}$ with $Q(t)=tq'+q''$ and $x(t)=(a-t)d+e$, proves positivity, $\log G=T+S$, the logarithmic adjacent-difference bridge, and unique minimization at $m$ on $\{1,\dots,2m\}$ under C22's quantitative hypotheses. Identifying the parameters with the continued fraction of $\log_2 3$ and Collatz dynamics, and full C64/C64b hump-law assembly, remain \texttt{[CONDITIONAL]} and unformalized.%
}{Preprint in preparation\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace\href{https://github.com/derekblair/math-proofs/blob/master/lean/docs/002.md}{Lean scope}}

\resultentry{003}{Erd\H{o}s--Straus witness-complex and QR-core theory}{%
Thirteen modules (spanning \texttt{NumberTheory/} and \texttt{Topology/WitnessComplex*}) prove witness-complex arithmetic (\texttt{b0\_vacuity}, \texttt{fiber\_collision\_law}, Thm~1(a) and Thm~3), T1/T2 prime-square obstructions, 5/7 forcing, descent transfer kernels, and a pair-specific SDR endgame. \texttt{ESCovering.lean} constructs positive natural denominators from splitting premises; all three mod-7 universal families, T1 classes at $w=20$, and T2 fibers at $q=7$ export full covering statements. \texttt{ESQRCoreCount.qr\_residue\_count} proves $|Q|=|S|/2^k$ for $M=24\prod_{p\in s}p$ with constructed CRT equivalences. \texttt{ESDescentCovering.lean} supplies branch-2 denominator algebra and composes the $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$ transfers with positive parent-progression identities under explicit slope/intercept normalizations. External filtered-frontier identification, cubic exclusion, external coverage tables, and the R74 sweep remain unformalized.%
}{Preprint in preparation\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace\href{https://github.com/derekblair/math-proofs/blob/master/lean/docs/003.md}{Lean scope}}

\resultentry{005}{$q$-adic classification of Erd\H{o}s--Straus witnesses}{%
\textbf{QA-001} (\texttt{[CONDITIONAL]}): \texttt{ESQadicBranch1.qadic\_type\_restriction} proves $v_q(r)\le v_q(A)$ under explicit QQ-integrality, M23 divisibility, and R74 no-descended-bundle inputs, alongside kernel-checked arithmetic kills (\texttt{kill\_02}, \texttt{kill\_12}, \texttt{descent\_01}). \texttt{ESQadicBoundary.lean} proves $\alpha\le1$ and $\rho\le1$ without the sweep premise, derives $q\nmid m$ from M23, and checks a generic type-$(0,1)$ witness showing R74 cannot simply be deleted (without refuting the fixed refined-target claim). The external R74 sweep and framework supplying QQ/M23 inputs remain unformalized.%
}{Preprint in preparation\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace\href{https://github.com/derekblair/math-proofs/blob/master/lean/docs/005.md}{Lean scope}}
