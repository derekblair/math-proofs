\begin{abstract}
This document describes the result families in the \texttt{math-proofs}
collection: machine-checked mathematics in which every proven result ships as
a paper preprint (human-readable argument) paired with its Lean~4
formalization (machine-checked proof). Manuscripts are organized into
families; each family groups a principal result with companion arguments,
consequences, or alternative proofs. All formalizations typecheck with
\texttt{lake build} under Lean~4.34.1 + Mathlib~v4.34.1. The per-theorem
verification status and axiom dependencies are recorded in
\texttt{formalization.yaml} (machine-readable) and
\texttt{FORMALIZATION\_CATALOGUE.md} (human-readable).
\end{abstract}

\section{Family 001 --- Erd\H{o}s--Straus identities and obstructions}

Results on representing $4/n$ as a sum of three unit fractions.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
ID & Result & Status & Lean module \\
\midrule
ES-001 & New covering identity for $n \equiv 217 \pmod{264}$ & \texttt{[PROVED]} & \texttt{ESIdentity.lean} \\
ES-002 & Finite-identity barrier on square classes mod~840 & \texttt{[PROVED*]} & \texttt{ESBarrier.lean} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Family 001 manuscripts.}
\end{table}

\textbf{ES-001} gives a new covering identity
\[
\frac{4}{n} = \frac{1}{(n+3)/4} + \frac{1}{n(n+3)} + \frac{11}{n(n+3)},
\qquad n \equiv 217 \pmod{264},
\]
covering the prime $1009$ --- the smallest prime missed by the published
Mordell/Yamamoto/Rosati identities. The formalized theorem is
\texttt{es\_identity\_217mod264}, depending only on Lean's standard axioms
(\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}).

\textbf{ES-002} proves that no finite system of polynomial identities
$4/(A_i t + B_i) = \sum 1/F_{ij}(t)$ with positive leading coefficients can
cover a full square class mod~840: infinitely many primes escape every such
system. Witness: $p = 77{,}598{,}049 \equiv 529 \pmod{840}$. The
formalization carries two explicitly labeled classical-input axioms:
\texttt{schinzel\_mordell} and \texttt{dirichlet\_arith\_prog}.

\section{Family 002 --- Collatz convergent control (gap-factor theory)}

A machine-checked theory of the gap factor $G(t)$ on from-above blocks of
$\log_2 3$'s continued fraction, developed across thirteen Lean modules:
the complete-quotient API for irrationals (\texttt{CollatzGapCF}),
the C24 tail-mirror selector and strict dichotomy (\texttt{CollatzGapC24},
\texttt{CollatzGapC24Lemma3}), C23 sinh estimates
(\texttt{CollatzGapC23}, \texttt{CollatzGapSinh}), the C22/C25/C26 assembly
(\texttt{CollatzGapC22C25}), C18b convexity and the C26 margin-duality
trichotomy (\texttt{CollatzGapConvex}), C29 discrepancy dominance and its
from-below mirror (\texttt{CollatzGapC29}, \texttt{CollatzGapC29Mirror}),
and the C64/C64b hump-law identity kernel (\texttt{CollatzGapHump}).
All modules are \texttt{[PROVED]} with standard axioms only; preprints are in
preparation.

\section{Family 003 --- Erd\H{o}s--Straus witness-complex and QR-core theory}

A machine-checked theory of Erd\H{o}s--Straus covering families on refined
moduli: the witness complex $W(M)$ (\texttt{WitnessComplexThm2},
\texttt{WitnessComplexThm13}), the T2 prime-square obstruction
(\texttt{ESPrimeSquareT2}), the T1 two-case theorem (\texttt{EST1TwoCase}),
and the QR-core --- the set of quadratic-residue classes missed by both
covering systems --- with its exact count $|Q| = |S|/2^k$
(\texttt{ESQRCore}, \texttt{ESQRCoreCount}), the 5/7-forcing lemma
(\texttt{ESFiveSevenForcing}), the mod-7 universal families and
fiber-pinning lemma (\texttt{ESMod7UniversalFamilies}), the X0/S1S3 descent
core (\texttt{ESX0S1S3}), general descent transfer
(\texttt{ESDescentTransfer}), and the pair-specific conditional SDR endgame
(\texttt{ESBranchSDREndgame}). All modules are \texttt{[PROVED]} with
standard axioms only; preprints are in preparation.

\section{Family 004 --- Girth-5 extremal graphs}

\textbf{G5-001} is the 3v floor theorem for girth-5 graphs
(\texttt{Girth5Floor3v.lean}, \texttt{[PROVED]}): the emitted-graph
re-attachment model and cross-edge harmlessness argument, showing that no
improving 3v move exists on the extremal graphs. Preprint in preparation.

\section{Family 005 --- q-adic classification of Erd\H{o}s--Straus witnesses}

\textbf{QA-001} is the branch-1 q-adic type theorem
(\texttt{ESQadicBranch1.lean}, \texttt{[PROVED]}): $v_q(r) \le v_q(A)$ on
refined targets, the first classification theorem for the q-adic types of
Erd\H{o}s--Straus witnesses. Preprint in preparation.

\section{Family 006 --- Saturated Sidon sets}

\textbf{SS-001} (\texttt{[SOLVED]}): the set
$\{16, 62, 66, 74, 88, 104, 117, 122, 123\}$ is Sidon and saturated in
$[1,184]$, verified on three independent code paths. Paper:
\texttt{papers/r47-sat184-October-7-2026/}.
