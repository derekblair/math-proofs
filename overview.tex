\begin{abstract}
This document describes the result families in the \texttt{math-proofs}
collection of Lean~4 formalizations and accompanying manuscripts. The exported
Lean statements determine the proved scope. Some larger research claims still
require identifications, external inputs, or assemblies that are not formalized;
those boundaries are stated below. The project pins Lean~4.34.1 and
Mathlib~v4.34.1. Selected theorem names, statuses, and axiom dependencies appear
in \texttt{formalization.yaml} and \texttt{FORMALIZATION\_CATALOGUE.md}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Family 001 --- Erd\H{o}s--Straus identities and obstructions}

Results on representing $4/n$ as a sum of three unit fractions.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
ID & Result & Status & Lean module \\
\midrule
ES-001 & Positive natural covering witnesses for $n \equiv 217 \pmod{264}$ & \texttt{[PROVED]} & \texttt{ESIdentity.lean} \\
ES-002 & CRT escape from supplied local obstructions & \texttt{[PROVED]} & \texttt{ESBarrier.lean} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Family 001 manuscripts.}
\end{table}

\textbf{ES-001} proves that every natural $n\equiv217\pmod{264}$ has
positive natural denominators $x,y,z$ with
\[
4x=n+3,\qquad y=n(n+3),\qquad 11z=n(n+3),\qquad
\frac4n=\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z.
\]
The theorem \texttt{es\_covering\_217mod264} includes positivity,
integrality, the denominator equations, and the unit-fraction identity.
The compatible rational-equality lemma is
\texttt{es\_identity\_217mod264}; both use standard Lean axioms only.
The example $n=1009$ is included in the module.
\texttt{ESIdentityCRT.lean} proves the exact six covered subclasses modulo
9240, positive covering witnesses for their members, and exactly one out
of eleven subclasses of each parent residue modulo 840. This is a residue
count, not a prime-density or historical-priority theorem.
The former novelty claim is withdrawn in light of Lopez (2024),
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2206.10319v4}{arXiv:2206.10319v4};
the corrected ES-001 manuscript records the prior art and specialization.

\textbf{ES-002} exports \texttt{es\_crt\_escape}: for finitely many
progressions with $8\mid A_i$ and an explicit local obstruction for each
$B_i$, infinitely many primes in each listed square class modulo~840 avoid
every progression. The supplied obstruction is either an odd prime
$q\mid A_i$ for which $B_i$ is a nonsquare modulo $q$, or $B_i\not\equiv1
\pmod8$. The CRT argument and Mathlib's general Dirichlet theorem give a
proof with no custom axioms. The former inconsistent assumptions and the
false rational-polynomial \texttt{es\_barrier} statement were removed.
The Schinzel--Mordell implication from integer polynomial identities to
these local obstructions is not formalized; no polynomial-identity barrier
is asserted as an end-to-end Lean theorem.

\section{Family 002 --- Collatz convergent control (gap-factor theory)}

Twelve modules prove general continued-fraction identities, analytic lemmas,
and conditional argmin assemblies: the complete-quotient API
(\texttt{CollatzGapCF}); C24 selectors and first-difference rules
(\texttt{CollatzGapC24}, \texttt{CollatzGapC24Lemma3}); analytic estimates
and comparison helpers (\texttt{CollatzGapSinh}, \texttt{CollatzGapDelta});
discrete monotonicity and C26 margin duality (\texttt{CollatzGapConvex});
C23 and C22/C25/C26 assemblies (\texttt{CollatzGapC23},
\texttt{CollatzGapC22C25}); discrepancy bounds
(\texttt{CollatzGapC29}, \texttt{CollatzGapC29Mirror}); and the hump-law
identity kernel (\texttt{CollatzGapHump}). These named statements are
\texttt{[PROVED]} with standard axioms only.

The repaired \texttt{GapCF.c26c} gives both selector equivalences, with
its quantitative $K(n)$ hypothesis required only when $\rho<r$.
\texttt{c22\_upper} and \texttt{c22\_argmin} now export the conditional
odd-block upper bound and argmin equality. \texttt{c29\_strict} exports
strict interior discrepancy positivity, and the argmin helper includes
membership of the selected index in its domain.

\texttt{CollatzGapFactor.lean} defines the explicit product
\[
G(t)=\frac{Q(t+1)}{Q(t)}\frac{2^{x(t)}-1}{2^{x(t+1)}-1},\qquad
Q(t)=tq'+q'',\quad x(t)=(a-t)d+e.
\]
It proves positivity, $\log G=T+S$, and the logarithmic adjacent-difference
bridge. Under C22's existing quantitative hypotheses, the factor has a
unique minimum at $m$ on $\{1,\ldots,2m\}$; the bridge and existence of a
minimizer are proved. Identifying the parameters with the continued fraction
of $\log_2 3$ and Collatz dynamics remains unassembled.
The full C64/C64b hump laws also require further assembly beyond the
exported identities and bounds. These larger interpretations are
\texttt{[CONDITIONAL]}, not completed concrete Collatz theorems.

\section{Family 003 --- Erd\H{o}s--Straus witness-complex and QR-core theory}

The modules prove witness-complex arithmetic, T1/T2 square obstructions,
5/7 forcing, descent kernels, and a pair-specific SDR endgame.
\texttt{ESCovering.lean} now constructs positive natural denominators from
the splitting premises. All three mod-7 universal families, T1 classes
at $w=20$, and T2 fibers at $q=7$ export complete covering statements,
including the first member of each progression.

\texttt{ESQRCoreCount.qr\_residue\_count} proves $|Q|=|S|/2^k$ for
$M=24\prod_{p\in s}p$, where $s$ is a finite set of primes at least five,
$S$ is the unit residues congruent to one modulo 24, and $Q$ is its subset
of squares modulo $M$. Both CRT equivalences and the equivalence of square
solvability are proved. This does not identify an externally filtered
frontier with $S$. Cubic exclusion, external coverage tables, and R74
sweep data remain outside these statements.
\texttt{ESDescentCovering.lean} proves the branch-2 denominator algebra
and composes the $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$ arithmetic transfers with positive
parent-progression identities. The required slope, intercept, and parameter
normalizations remain explicit; a classification of all external identities
into these normalizations is not proved. Preprints are in preparation.

\section{Family 004 --- Girth-5 extremal graphs}

\textbf{G5-001} (\texttt{Girth5Floor3v.lean}) proves local reattachment
kernels using corrected feasibility conditions: attachment containment,
guards on candidate internal edges, and restrictions for internal two-edge
paths. The identity move is feasible and attains score $D$, yielding the
floor. For every feasible move on a distinct triple,
\texttt{feasible\_emit\_noTri} and \texttt{feasible\_emit\_noC4}
prove general cycle exclusion, and \texttt{emittedGraph} constructs a
simple graph of girth at least five. Five invalid-move regressions and two
identity examples are included in \texttt{Girth5Floor3vRegression.lean}.
\texttt{Girth5Extremal.lean} counts unordered edges and proves
$e(G')+D=e(G)+\mathrm{score}$ for every feasible replacement.
Global maximality of $e(G)$ among all girth-five graphs on the vertex type
therefore implies the score ceiling. The new rigidity and gap theorems
conclude maximum score $D$ and gap $-1$ without assuming that ceiling.
Preprint in preparation.

\section{Family 005 --- q-adic classification of Erd\H{o}s--Straus witnesses}

\textbf{QA-001} is a \texttt{[CONDITIONAL]} classification:
\texttt{ESQadicBranch1.qadic\_type\_restriction} proves
$v_q(r)\le v_q(A)$ under its explicit QQ-integrality, M23 divisibility,
and R74 no-descended-bundle inputs. The arithmetic kills and descent
lemmas are kernel-checked using standard axioms. The external R74 sweep
and the full framework supplying the QQ/M23 inputs are not proved here.
\texttt{ESQadicBoundary.lean} proves $\alpha\le1$ and $\rho\le1$
without the sweep premise and derives $q\nmid m$ from the supplied M23
congruence. A fully checked generic type-$(0,1)$ example shows that simply
deleting R74 would be false; it does not refute the fixed refined-target
claim. Preprint in preparation.

\section{Family 006 --- Saturated Sidon sets}

\textbf{SS-001} (\texttt{[PROVED]}):
\texttt{SidonSat184.r47\_sat184} proves that
$\{16,62,66,74,88,104,117,122,123\}$ has nine elements and is a Sidon
subset of $[1,184]$ with no admissible extension in that interval. The
finite checks are evaluated by Lean's kernel, with standard axioms only.
\texttt{unique\_extension\_saturated} supplies the general extension lemma.
\texttt{SidonSat184Census.lean} additionally checks the nine displayed
one-hole rows, their 183 blocked positions, the saturated ten-element
extensions, and both C28 gate counterexamples.
Ancillary searches remain separate external computations. Search
exhaustiveness, minimality, and priority claims are not conclusions of
this theorem. Paper:
\texttt{papers/r47-sat184-October-7-2026/}.

\section{Family 014 --- ES absorption order and forcing spectrum}

\textbf{ES-005} (\texttt{[PROVED]}): the ES absorption poset and
redundancy rank --- A1--A3, disjoint superadditivity R3$'$, its general
corollary R3$''$, the overlap-deficit bound T, absorption collapse R4$'$,
and singleton factorization (\texttt{ESAbsorptionOrder.lean}). The datum
equivalence assumes $C_G\ne\emptyset$; for distinct $G\preceq H$, absorption
collapse and its unique minimum core assume $C_H\ne\emptyset$. Singleton
factorization uses a finite ambient family $\mathcal G\supseteq S$, with $C_g\ne\{c\}$ for all
$c\in S_0$ and $g\in\mathcal G$. Also proved are the forcing
spectrum partition S2(a)$'$/S2(b--d)/S3/S4
(\texttt{ESForcingSpectrum.lean}). For the forcing decomposition,
\texttt{ESForcingDecomp.lean} checks A's finite membership
$((H-1)/24)\bmod35\in\mathrm{OpenKClasses}$ for $H\in\mathrm{HardClasses}$
and, separately for natural $k<35$, the equivalence
$(k\bmod7\in\{0,1,5\}\land k\bmod5\in\{0,2\})\iff
k\in\mathrm{OpenKClasses}$. B and C are the scaling and filter lemmas
under their stated hypotheses. D proves only
$35/\gcd(m,35)\in\{1,5,7,35\}$ for $m>0$; the exclusion of
$\{r,r+7,r+14,r+21,r+28\}$ from being a subset of
$\mathrm{OpenKClasses}$ for natural $r<7$; and
$|f.\mathrm{covered}|=1\Rightarrow35\mid m$, assuming
$|f.\mathrm{covered}|=35/\gcd(m,35)$ and $m=f.\mathrm{mprime}>0$.
The latter lemma's additional \texttt{hC} premise is tautological.
A's general congruence export and D's covered-set/progression bridge,
cardinality-formula derivation, and surgical prime-covering/hard-cell
biconditional assembly remain \texttt{[UNFORMALIZED]}. Paper:
\url{papers/es-absorption-forcing-October-11-2026/}.
