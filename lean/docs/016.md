## Scope

### C064: Refutation of Karttunen's $n^3$ bound on row quotients of OEIS `A391736` (`[PROVED]`)

One module under `MathProofs/SeamB/C064/` is kernel-checked with standard axioms only (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), zero `sorry`, zero `native_decide`, and independent `leanchecker` replay (`exit=0`):

- `MathProofs/SeamB/C064/Solution.lean`:
  - Defines carryless base-2 multiplication `clMul` (`OEIS A048720`) and proves exact equivalence with $\mathbb{F}_2[X]$ polynomial bit-convolution (`clMul_testBit`).
  - Establishes general LSB-to-MSB carry-automaton soundness (`carryEq_parity`, `carryEq_zero`, `carryEq_step_even`, `carryEq_step_odd`).
  - Certifies via a pure-kernel 73-state indexed reachability table (`checkCert_eq_true : checkCert = true := rfl`) that no integer $i \in [1, 2^{28})$ satisfies $215 \cdot i = 239 \otimes i$ (`no_sol_lt_2pow28`).
  - Certifies $215 \cdot 302{,}775{,}165 = 65{,}096{,}660{,}475 = 239 \otimes 302{,}775{,}165$ (`clMul_239_302775165`), proving `c064_witness`, `c064_refutation_card` (`¬ ∀ n ≥ 1, (rowA391736Values n).ncard = 1 + A115861 n`), `c064_refutation_pointwise`, and `c064_refutation_doubling` (`¬ ∀ n ≥ 1, A115861 (2 * n) = A115861 n`, failing at $n = 478$).
  - Also proves Karttunen's general lower bound `rowA391736_card_ge` and verifies secondary product identities `c064_secondary_n4` ($n = 391 > 391^4$) and `c064_secondary_n5` ($n = 511 > 511^5$).

Global minimality of $n = 239$ across all $n < 239$ and the 384-pair landscape for odd $n \le 3000$ are `[COMPUTED]` (verified by `automaton_c064.c`, `search_c064.c`, and `verify_counterexample.py`), not formalized in Lean.
