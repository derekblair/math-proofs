## Scope

### SS-004: Generalized sub-family identity and march ladder ceilings (`[PROVED]` / staged `[PROVED*]`)

Two modules under `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/` are kernel-checked with standard axioms only:

- `ESSubfamilyExtended.lean`: flagship `ESSubfamilyExtended.generalized_subfamily_identity` (`[PROVED]`) plus staged `[PROVED*]` extensions `admission_criterion`, `a0_constancy`, `u_eq_Aqe_div_f`, `excess_prime_dvd_u`, `M_const`, and repaired conditional `split_layered_repaired`. External shape censuses (`781,250` shapes, `60` ladders) and catalog ledgers remain `[COMPUTED]` data and are unformalized.
