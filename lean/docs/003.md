# Erdős–Straus witness-complex and QR-core theory

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization for Family 003 (preprint in preparation).

## Scope

### ES-003: Witness-complex, QR-core, covering, and descent arithmetic kernels (`[PROVED]`)

Thirteen modules (eleven under `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/` and two under `Mathproofs/Topology/`) are kernel-checked with standard axioms only:

- `Mathproofs/Topology/WitnessComplexThm2.lean`: `b0_vacuity`, `fiber_collision_law`.
- `Mathproofs/Topology/WitnessComplexThm13.lean`: `t2_integrality_iff`, `t3_full_class` (7 theorems total covering W(M) Thm 1(a) and Thm 3).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESPrimeSquareT2.lean`: `t2_jacobi_neg_one`, `t2_class_nonsquare` (T2 prime-square obstruction).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/EST1TwoCase.lean`: `t1_two_case` (T1 `x² ≡ -f` unsolvability via two-case reciprocity).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESQRCore.lean`: `qr_core_t2_misses`, `qr_core_t1_misses` (T1 and T2 residue obstructions).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESQRCoreCount.lean`: `qr_core_count`, `residueCRT`, `targetCRTEquiv`, `qrCRTEquiv`, `qr_residue_count`, `qr_residue_count_finset` — proves `|Q| = |S| / 2^k` for actual unit residues congruent to 1 modulo 24 and their full-modulus square subset modulo `24 * ∏ p` (`p ≥ 5` distinct primes).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESCovering.lean`: `of_split`, `splitting_family`, `of_t2` (positive natural Egyptian-fraction covering constructors).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESFiveSevenForcing.lean`: `t1_five_forcing_full`, `t2_seven_forcing`, `t1_five_covers_class`, `t2_seven_covers_class`, `t1_w20_class_covered`, `qnr7_covered` (positive natural covering for w=20 T1 classes and q=7 T2 fibers).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESMod7UniversalFamilies.lean`: `fam1_universal/fam2_universal/fam3_universal`, `fiber_pin`, `fam1_covers`, `fam2_covers`, `fam3_covers` (full positive natural covering witnesses for all three mod-7 universal families).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESX0S1S3.lean`: `lemma_X0`, `lemma_S1S3`.
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESDescentTransfer.lean`: `mu_valuation_of_S3`, `transfer_key_00`, `transfer_qcancel`.
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESDescentCovering.lean`: `branch2_pointwise`, `branch2_covering_of_divisibility`, `descend_intercept`, `transfer_00_covering`, `transfer_11_covering` (branch-2 denominator algebra and positive parent-progression identities under explicit slope/intercept normalizations).
- `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/ESBranchSDREndgame.lean`: `branch_sdr_endgame`, `branch_sdr_endgame_clique_bound`.

Cubic-family exclusion, identification of externally filtered frontiers with `targetResidues`, external 165-class coverage sweeps and fiber censuses, framework normalization of arbitrary external branch identities, and the R74 sweep-contradiction source remain unformalized.
