## Scope

### SS-001: Saturated 9-element Sidon witness and one-hole census (`[PROVED]`)

Two modules under `Mathproofs/Combinatorics/` are kernel-checked with standard axioms only (no `native_decide` or external certificates):

- `SidonSat184.lean`: proves `SidonSat184.r47_sat184` — the set `astar = {16, 62, 66, 74, 88, 104, 117, 122, 123}` has cardinality 9, lies in `[1, 184]`, satisfies the Sidon property (including diagonal pairs), and admits no Sidon extension in `[1, 184]` — together with the general extension theorem `SidonSat184.unique_extension_saturated`.
- `SidonSat184Census.lean`: proves `census_facts`, `census_unique_hole`, `census_blocked_card`, `census_extensions_saturated`, and `census_gate_counterexamples` for all nine displayed one-hole rows (rows 13–21), their 183 blocked positions, saturated ten-element extensions, and the two C28 gate counterexamples (rows 14 and 21 excluding 121 and 123).

Large shell enumerations, earlier census rows, search exhaustiveness, minimality, and historical priority claims remain unformalized (ancillary search is `[COMPUTED]`).
