## Scope

### ES-005: Absorption order, redundancy collapse, forcing spectrum, and partial decomposition (`[PROVED]`)

Three modules under `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/` are kernel-checked with standard axioms only:

- `ESAbsorptionOrder.lean`: proves `absorp_refl/trans/antisymm` (A1), `datum_eq_of_absorpLE` (without nonemptiness), `absorp_datum_iff` (assuming `G.C.Nonempty`), `rho_superadd_disjoint` (R3′), `rho_superadd_general` (R3″), `overlap_deficit_bound` (T), `rho_absorption_collapse` and `absorption_collapse_unique_core` (for distinct `G ≼ H` with `H.C.Nonempty`), and `singleton_factorization` (taking a finite ambient family `𝒢 ⊇ S` with no singleton group on any cell of `S0` within `𝒢`).
- `ESForcingSpectrum.lean`: proves `s2a'_forced`, `s2b_pc`, `s2c_void`, `s2d_split`, `spectrum_partition`, and `psi_union_max`.
- `ESForcingDecomp.lean`: `theoremA_crt` checks `(H - 1) / 24 % 35 ∈ OpenKClasses` for `H ∈ HardClasses`; `theoremA_crt_box` separately proves `(k % 7 ∈ {0,1,5} ∧ k % 5 ∈ {0,2}) ↔ k ∈ OpenKClasses` for natural `k < 35`; `theoremB_scaling` and `theoremC_filter` prove the scaling and filter lemmas under their stated hypotheses; partial `theoremD_card` lemmas prove `35 / gcd(m, 35) ∈ {1, 5, 7, 35}` for `m > 0`, exclude `{r, r+7, r+14, r+21, r+28} ⊆ OpenKClasses` for `r < 7`, and prove `f.covered.card = 1 → 35 ∣ f.mprime` assuming `f.covered.card = 35 / gcd(f.mprime, 35)` and `0 < f.mprime` (its additional `hC` premise is tautological).

External group censuses (`78` union groups, `7,052,450` minimal u-covers), the cover-greedoid material, Theorem A's general congruence export, and Theorem D's covered-set/progression bridge, cardinality-formula derivation, and surgical prime-covering/hard-cell biconditional assembly remain unformalized. The surgical down-set A4 remains open.
