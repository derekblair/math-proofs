## Scope

### SS-006: Repaired abstract lock laws and transition-DAG dual-ascent barrier (`[PROVED]`)

Two modules under `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/` are kernel-checked with standard axioms only:

- `AbstractLock.lean`: `consecT_gives_consec_fire`, `no_consecT_scattered`, `consec_window_no_consecT_not_full`, `full_no_consecT_gap_window`, and `sharpness_witness` (`W = {0, 2}` counterexample to the original unconditional T3(iii) claim).

The original full D7 barrier (`[OPEN]`), census transfer results C1/C2/C4 (`[OPEN]`), and the `472`-frame / `24/16/14`-edge censuses remain unformalized.
