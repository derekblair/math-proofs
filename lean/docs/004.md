# Girth-5 extremal graphs

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization for Family 004 (preprint in preparation).

## Scope

### G5-001: The 3v floor, rigidity, and gap theorems (`[PROVED]`)

Three modules under `Mathproofs/Combinatorics/` are kernel-checked with standard axioms only:

- `Girth5Floor3v.lean`: defines `Girth5Graph`, corrected local reattachment feasibility `IsFeasible` (attachment containment, candidate internal-edge guards, internal two-edge-path exclusions), `emittedGraph`, and proves `identity_feasible`, `score_identity`, `three_v_floor`, conditional `three_v_rigidity`, `cross_edge_no_triangle`, `cross_edge_no_c4`, `feasible_emit_noTri`, and `feasible_emit_noC4`. Every feasible move on a distinct triple emits a simple graph of girth at least 5.
- `Girth5Floor3vRegression.lean`: rejects five invalid moves (`triangle_move_rejected`, `loop_move_rejected`, `four_cycle_move_rejected`, `cross_edge_move_rejected`, `path_bad_move_rejected`) and accepts two identity moves (`empty_identity_feasible`, `path_identity_feasible`).
- `Girth5Extremal.lean`: counts ordinary unordered `SimpleGraph.edgeFinset` edges (`emitted_edgeCount_balance`) and derives `score_le_D_of_extremal`, `three_v_rigidity_of_extremal`, and `three_v_gap_of_extremal` (maximum feasible score equals `D` and three-vertex gap equals `−1`) from global edge maximality among all girth-at-least-5 graphs on the same vertex type.

The accompanying manuscript remains in preparation.
