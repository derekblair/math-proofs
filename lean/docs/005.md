# q-adic classification of Erdős–Straus witnesses

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization for Family 005 (preprint in preparation).

## Scope

### QA-001: Conditional branch-1 q-adic type theorem (`[CONDITIONAL]`)

Two modules under `Mathproofs/NumberTheory/` are kernel-checked with standard axioms only:

- `ESQadicBranch1.lean`: proves `ESQadicBranch1.qadic_type_restriction` (`v_q(r) ≤ v_q(A)` assuming explicit QQ-integrality, M23 divisibility and `q`-free hypotheses, and the R74 no-descended-bundle exclusion) together with the arithmetic lemmas `kill_02`, `kill_12`, and `descent_01`.
- `ESQadicBoundary.lean`: proves `branch1_valuation_bounds`, `qadic_type_bounds_without_sweep` (`α ≤ 1` and `ρ ≤ 1` without R74), `qadic_type_restriction_from_m23` (deriving `q ∤ m` from M23), and `generic_type01_counterexample` (a concrete generic type-`(0,1)` witness demonstrating that R74 cannot simply be deleted without additional refined-target hypotheses; this does not refute the fixed 2042040/refined-target claim).

The external R74 sweep and the full framework supplying the QQ/M23 inputs for refined targets remain unformalized.
