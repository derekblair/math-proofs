# Independent multiread topology audit

**Verdict:** The fixed-embedding rank criterion, field-uniform characterization, and one-deletion optimum are mathematically sound in the stated ideal passive model. Add an explicit assumption that public committee/site/read/corruption choices and the set of visible messages are fixed or independent of secret and hidden state. Committing the helper set before message generation alone does not rule out a selector that already depends on the private shares. State 0≤c≤k≤n, k=t+1, and sufficient eligible labels for each instance. These results do not ensure privacy from private-state-correlated selection, other reads, cross-repair transcripts, active adversaries, postcommit disappearance or deployment.

**Fixed embedding.** Nonzero interpolation weights at the target produce an invertible coordinate change. For equal visible traffic and exposed information, true weighted-coordinate differences lie precisely in the hidden edge incidence span, before imposing the external read restrictions. The visible target sum is automatic under those zero-sum directions. Difference masks provide matching translations of the uniform input-and-hidden-pad fiber, proving indistinguishability for every pair of secret values when the secret functional is nonzero on the read kernel inside that span. If zero, the corresponding affine-view image is disjoint for different secrets (indeed the secret is determined linearly). Restricting to incidence columns yields the edge rows. Normalization removes only nonzero row and column scalars. Thus failure on some embedding is not failure on each embedding: in F7 with a=1, b=2 and edge matching (0,1),(2,3), labels (3,5,4,6) are safe and (3,4,5,6) are unsafe. Both are admissible in the same field with the same target and read.

**Uniform sufficiency.** If the secret edge row belongs to the span of the read rows, the corresponding linear combination of Lagrange ratio functions is constant on each connected component. Over the common denominator D of degree d=r+1 the numerator has degree ≤d and nonzero value at zero because the secret coefficient supplies a nonzero pole there and no read has one. For any constant c, the nonzero polynomial N−cD therefore has at most d distinct eligible roots; a component with r+2 sites is enough. No clique is needed.

**Uniform necessity.** Even for fixed eligible a and read sites in a base finite field, D is squarefree (all its listed roots are distinct), so the derivative is nonzero, also in small characteristic. The algebraic closure has infinitely many values and only finitely many critical values; avoid also 0 and D(a). Each separately chosen fiber then has exactly d distinct points, and distinct fibers do not intersect. A finite collection of selected points, values, and enough further unused helper labels lies in one finite extension. Partial fractions give a nonzero 0-residue of 1/D. Scale it to match the actual nonzero secret Lagrange residue, then match all read residues by arbitrary coefficients of their respective ratios (nonzero a−b factors). Remainder is constant since it has no finite poles and is bounded at infinity. Consequently the combination is constant on every selected component, so the fixed-field rank test fails on that constructed embedding. This proves necessity **over all eligible finite fields and assignments**, not on any particular field.

**Graph claims.** Every h=k−c subset can be realized as the honest set for an eligible k-set, and containment in a component of size ≥r+2 survives induction to larger honest sets. h=r+2 reduces to ordinary connectivity after deleting n−h vertices; h=n requires a tree of r+1 edges on one component. For h=n−1, d≥2, and n≥d+2, a cycle on d+2 vertices works. At most d+1 edges either allow a component of d+2 vertices (necessarily a tree exhausting the budget, invalidated by deleting a nonleaf), or allow at most one component of d+1 vertices since 2d>d+1 (invalidated by deleting any of its vertices), or already have no adequate component. This establishes r+3 without implying a general graph optimum.

Independent check: reran checks.py: 3,072 graph/field instances and one-deletion optima 4,5,6 at six vertices. The Lean log shows only standard axioms for three supporting algebra lemmas. It does **not** formalize the entire privacy reduction, finite-extension argument, or extremal graph conclusions. The linked secure repair / aggregation and graph results are context; this audit does not certify novelty or priority.
