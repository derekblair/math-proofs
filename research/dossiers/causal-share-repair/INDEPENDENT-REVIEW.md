# Independent review: causal repair-only entropy

**Verdict:** The paper entropy optimization checks under its explicit fixed schedule, independent additive zero-sum pads, and pointwise prefix-simulation quantifier. It is not a lower bound on all protocols for secret-private repair. The result is classical, information theoretic, and under the ideal share-read model, not endpoint-compromise or cryptosystem security.

- On any observed prefix, closeness for two allowable true histories with equal target values is exactly translation invariance by the difference subspace K_e. This uses bijectivity from t+1 *distinct* helper evaluations and nonzero weights. Arbitrary nonuniform independent pads do not evade the chain-rule bound: within every earlier-pad conditioning event of positive mass the fresh-direction subgroup acts by translations on the next block and gives uniform mass within each orbit. Joint conditioning on true future histories is unnecessary and would be wrong.
- The backwards construction's invariant is equality of the projection at the moment a level is processed, not necessarily equality in the final W. Subsequent shorter-prefix padding still preserves inclusion, which is the sufficiency criterion. The zero-tail complement has trivial intersection with the existing W because its image at level e is complementary. The dimension increment therefore telescopes exactly to the lower bound. A simulator can factor each target-conditioned prefix law chronologically: projection to the earlier transcript uses only the earlier target shares by pointwise invariance. This argument handles adaptive ordinary reads only inside the expressly ideal snapshot interface; condition on the full true history when coupling simulation and answers to reads.
- The public sum really is the current target share. Equal targets across epochs do not imply equal target **values** unless the refresh directions vanish there. When they vanish, dividing by 1−X/a after removing the known target evaluation gives a bijection preserving constant terms and lowering the sharing degree by one. Necessity conditions on the initial target evaluation and every increment evaluation, leaving d_j−r_j independent residual directions. Cost t+2D_min is for separate independent pad and refresh entropy, not for protocols with joint or secret-dependent randomness. The two-distinct-target rank argument is rank two on the initial nonconstant directions exactly when a_1 and a_2 are distinct **nonzero** sites and t≥2.
- Small definition fix before release: explicitly require E≥1, a_e∈F_q×, and all shareholder site coordinates distinct and nonzero (hence q−1≥n). The prose currently says the helpers are nonzero, but the target is only an “endpoint.” If 0 were allowed as an endpoint, the repaired public sum would literally be the secret, and all secret-privacy/degree-reduction claims would fail (the isolated entropy theorem still works). State field-size qualification at the fixed-target and two-epoch headline; make clear generic direction-column selection either has full target dimension or can be extended to that dimension without destroying privacy.
- Locally reran rank_controls.py: 1,000 cases and three exact controls passed. They check dimensions and containments of selected linear pads, not arbitrary laws, simulator security or generic-field proof. The Lean log verifies precisely a support-fibre injection and rank-nullity lemma, only standard axioms; neither is a formal entropy theorem.

## Literature scope

[Huang–Bruck](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00500) and [Laing–Stinson](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7190) are direct secure-repair comparators, but their headline optimizations are bandwidth/share-rate rather than entropy of independent additive masks with a publicly visible target and pointwise online prefix privacy. [Proactive Secret Sharing with Constant Communication](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/942) compares a different communication/adversarial objective. The proof uses familiar entropy chain rules, subgroup-invariant pads, and rank-nullity; a targeted primary-source check did not find the exact causal formula but does not establish priority. No live probing, secrets, outbound communication, or credential use.
