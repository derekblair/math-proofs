# Online optimal padding for adaptively requested share repair

An ideal read-only model, not an implementation, credential protection claim or KEM. No real requests, endpoints, keys or deployed systems. This extends the audited fixed-schedule repair model while keeping a protected stateful pad sampler. It does **not** remove the confidential pad channel or protected storage.

## Information and timing model

Within an execution the secret is unchanged; across possible true histories it may vary. Exact repairs do not change the polynomial. For E≥1, the linear refresh directions U_j, of dimensions d_j, and their independent true coins are independent of repair actions and pads. Every share site and repair target is nonzero; the sites are distinct. The latent true history is never supplied to the pad sampler. A round action (target, ordered live helpers, publicly declared visible-message indices and authorized current-output policy) is chosen before that round's fresh pad coins, possibly based on all prior authorized values and observed masked messages. The actual selection policy is not known beforehand. Actions use no hidden sampler state. The legal action tree is finite, public, and fixed independently of the latent true history and any private pad state. Choices are nonanticipating: a stage action is fixed before that stage’s authorized outputs, visible messages, and fresh coins. A legal path is forcible if the observer can choose its actions regardless of observed values; the minimax lower bound assumes every legal path is forcible. Actions and visibility may differ by round; the sampler knows the current declaration but cannot anticipate a later one. External random choices of the observer are independent of primitive state.

With t+1 distinct live helpers excluding the nonzero target, Lagrange weights are nonzero and the full weighted helper vector determines the polynomial. For correctness each helper message receives its own zero-sum pad coordinate; the recovered value is their sum. Each round, the protected sampler privately delivers individual pad components to the relevant helper slots with ideal authenticated/confidential links that stay private under the stated stable-share reads. The repaired endpoint receives no pad seed and no special unmasking material. Each helper erases the used pad and sensitive work buffers. The sampler retains protected state sufficient for later sections (one simple implementation keeps the full prior visible-pad vector); this can grow with the history. Current true-share snapshot reads do not inspect sampler state, private helper pad traffic, helper work buffers, logs of erased shares or pad reuse domains. Any direct ordinary reads can instead be treated as separately authorized outputs in a chronological stage, if they are known to the public action policy, or left to the outside secret-privacy analysis. This is not whole-process corruption, active integrity, private-channel construction or a storage bound.

## Exact prefix criterion for mixed outputs and visibility

For a fixed legal action prefix of length e, let T_e be the linear map from the true history to all unmasked message coordinates that the observer is declared to see. Let L_e be the map to the sequence of outputs authorized for the simulator through e, such as selected true-share snapshots and designated repair sums. Declare a value only at the chronological stage at which disclosure is allowed, not at a stage where it is merely expected later. Additional direct observations can be separate stages with no new masked message coordinates. Let B_e be the product of the permitted spaces of visible pad vectors at each stage. For example, each full helper batch has sum-zero pad space; its projection on a strict subset of helpers is surjective because one private hidden component can balance the rest. The visibility map must be known when those pads are generated. Any designated hidden raw message uses a confidential or physically inaccessible route to the repaired holder. This is separate from private pad delivery. Hidden traffic, the recipient’s message and work buffers, helper buffers, and their logs must remain inaccessible to the observer; otherwise their contents are extra disclosures. The result does not cover selecting previously hidden messages retroactively.

Define K_e = T_e(ker L_e). This is the space of visible-message differences from two true histories with equal outputs through e. The truncation π drops the latest visible batch, and πK_e is a subspace of K_(e−1). For a fixed action prefix, perfect causal repair-only simulation is exactly invariance of the visible pad prefix law under translations by K_e, by comparing the message laws for every pair of such histories. It is impossible if K_e is not contained in B_e: a probability law supported on one subspace cannot be invariant under a vector outside it. For instance if the helper sum is excluded from the permitted outputs while every helper message is visible, the zero-sum-pad constraint cannot conceal that sum from the observer. This holds before any ordinary-secret privacy check; authorizing a value for simulation does not assert that exposing it meets a share corruption budget.

## Online extension: no extra randomness for adaptive action selection

Maintain a uniform visible-pad prefix on a public subspace W_(e−1) containing K_(e−1), for the corresponding forced action prefix. Assume K_e⊆B_e for legal actions. Upon learning the new action, let A_e be ker(π restricted to K_e), viewed as the fresh visible-coordinate directions with zero past. Choose a linear section s from πK_e to K_e of this projection. Extend it to a linear map s_bar:W_(e−1)→B_e with π(s_bar(w))=w. This is always possible by choosing a complement to πK_e and extending its basis with zero new coordinates. Then set

    W_e = s_bar(W_(e−1)) + A_e.

It contains K_e, projects exactly onto W_(e−1), and has direct-sum dimension dim W_(e−1)+dim A_e. For the next concrete pad, extend the actual old pad prefix w by s_bar(w)+a for a new independent uniform a∈A_e. Only a is sampled; the old pad coordinates are not changed. When visibility is partial, lift its newest projected pad through a fixed linear section of the allowed raw-to-visible projection to deliver full helper components. This lift needs no extra randomness and preserves correctness; hidden raw components must actually remain protected under the declared access model.

For any fixed action path, the resulting joint pad is uniform on W_e by induction. Even allowing nonuniform alternative pads, the fixed-path conditional entropy of the new visible batch given its prior values is at least dim A_e times log_2 q: translations by A_e act freely along each conditioned possible value and require equal mass on each orbit. Thus the online constructor **matches the optimum for every fixed path simultaneously**. More explicitly, the optimal worst-case number of fresh uniform field elements, assuming a finite horizon and each deterministic legal path can be forced by an allowed policy, is

    max_(legal paths) sum_e [dim K_e − dim πK_e],

with K_0=0. Charge all private randomness used by the sampler, including any seed or preloaded private randomness provided at setup. The upper uses no setup seed and draws exactly the displayed amount on each realized path. For an alternative independent private random source, the lower bounds its normalized Shannon entropy (divide by log_2 q) on the maximizing forced fixed path. The field-draw minimax is a worst-path bound, not an expected-draw optimum for variable-length sampling; a variable-length source transcript, including its stopping information, must be counted as a whole. Do not interpret the formula as the entropy *conditioned on an outcome-selected action path*: that conditioning can bias old pads. The number of new field elements drawn along a given realized action path is exactly its formula, not necessarily the entropy remaining undisclosed after conditioning on the path.

## Adaptive transcript proof, not an independence shortcut

Fix a true history h and the observer's private policy tape. For each forced deterministic prefix sigma of actions, denote by P_(sigma,h) the law of its visible message history under the construction with those actions forced. Because each update reads only the announced prefix and private past pad state, P_(sigma,h) marginalizes to the preceding forced-prefix law; without conditioning on later actions, forcing those actions cannot change earlier messages. The forced-prefix law depends on h only via y=L_sigma(h), because W_sigma contains K_sigma. Likewise, fresh coins are sampled after the action from a stream independent of the history and of all pre-round variables. Given the actually saved past pads and announced action, the only random input to the update is that fresh stream; no assumption is made that past pads are independent of true histories or uniformly distributed *conditional on the adaptive actions or observations*.

The simulator receives the legitimate stage-by-stage authorized outputs. It runs the same policy until that policy announces the next action. For any already emitted transcript m and actual authorized prefix y, choose any witness history h' with L_sigma(h')=y. Draw the next messages from the forced-prefix law for h' conditioned on its earlier messages m. This conditional law is well-defined on reached branches: its marginal equals the preceding forced law, and the canonical dependence on authorized outputs permits replacing h' with the actual h without changing the ratio. No future authorized value is required. For a candidate action path sigma, authorized values y and visible messages m, the adaptive prefix probability given h and the fixed policy tape is exactly the forced message probability P_(sigma,h)(m), multiplied by the indicator that the policy on those displayed (y,m) follows sigma and by the indicator that y=L_sigma(h). An incompatible path has adaptive probability zero; its forced message probability need not be zero. Proof at one step: prior actions and observations are fixed by the candidate prefix, so its next action is fixed before fresh coins; the next-message conditional probability is exactly the forced-prefix ratio above. Multiply these conditional probabilities inductively. The simulator and real protocol therefore agree on the distribution of every leaf and every prefix, including later direct reads selected from the simulated traffic. Declared reads can be incorporated as output-only stages with no pad draw; otherwise the same fixed-history true-share read oracle must supply their values in both real and simulated experiments. Finally average over the independent policy tape. This equality is for read-only classical observations, including quantum postprocessing of the same classical view; coherent lookup is not modeled.

## Shamir specialization and minimax

If all t+1 messages of a scheduled repair are visible and the one repaired value is authorized per round, then dim K_1=t. Projection-vanishing conditions on prior visible messages force all earlier coefficient polynomials to be zero (the nonzero-weight Lagrange coordinate maps are invertible). The new true-history change is just an increment in U_(e−1), with zero target evaluation at a_e. So the online cost of the newly sampled pad in round e≥2 is exactly

    d_(e−1) − rank(eval_(a_e) restricted to U_(e−1)).

This recovers the fixed-schedule optimum without predeclaring future targets; the argument works when both the targets and the live helper sets are adaptively chosen. If allowed target sets at each epoch are independent of each other, the minimax expression is t plus the sum of these per-epoch worst increments. A root of every direction in U_j, if eligible as a next target, maximizes that epoch's cost to d_j; otherwise the cost is d_j−1 for positive d_j. For restrictions linking targets across epochs, optimize that sum on the legal path graph; the same sampler already attains every path's cost. Cases with authorized extra direct snapshots or partial helper visibility use the general K_e formula rather than the simplified rank.

Example: with no refresh, t=2, two repairs and all helper messages visible, support for a future change of target cannot force more than two field pad coins. A fresh independent sum-zero mask per batch uses four in this setting. With a visible strict subset and target output intentionally not disclosed, safe masking is feasible because B_e is full on that subset; with all helper messages visible, withholding the output is impossible regardless of randomness. Earlier stationary and distinct-known refresh bounds concern privacy of the *authorized* trajectory and do not automatically extend to every adaptive trajectory. An adaptive selection can obtain different true shares over time, potentially exhausting the share budget; padding never conceals the authorized outputs themselves or strengthens the underlying proactive schedule.

## Exact protected-memory tradeoff in the stationary case

Consider the fixed, repeated target and helper order, no refresh, all helper messages visible, authorized repair sum each round. Let E≥1. The protected sampler starts with no random stored information, sees only the deterministic declarations, can retain at most q^m possible hidden states between repairs, and cannot reread earlier pads, messages, random tapes, helper storage, or any other external buffer. There is no hidden cache beyond the charged retained state; the deterministic public clock and declarations do not count as stored randomness. Count all its fresh private random field elements. For exact causal repair-only simulation the minimax budget now is

    t + (E−1) · max(0,t−m).

The equal-output difference K_e is the diagonal across epochs of a full t-dimensional sum-zero space. Its projection onto any round is that whole space, so each pad marginal must be uniform with entropy t log_2(q). For each later round the emitted pad is determined by the retained state and the new random input. Its entropy is at most the sum of their entropies; therefore at least max(0,t−m) new field elements are necessary every round. Achieve equality with a public basis of the single-epoch sum-zero space: choose min(t,m) basis coordinates once and retain them, then resample all other basis coordinates every round. This joint law is invariant under every diagonal shift, directly attaining causal simulation. Thus the t-coin optimum at m≥t does not mean protected storage is dispensable. If m=0, the exact necessary budget goes back to tE. This sharp bound does not cover general changing targets, shared external storage, or any public-traffic feedback into the sampler.

## Literature and scope

[Huang–Bruck](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00500) and [Laing–Stinson](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7190) study secure repair and bandwidth, with different adversarial and private-communication models; [their repairable threshold survey](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/1155) is a further comparator. This narrower causal-prefix simulator and exact minimax pad entropy are provisional; no absolute priority established. Rank lifting, entropy chain rules and Shamir interpolation themselves are standard. This is not a complete threshold ML-KEM, concealed-output service, malicious-secure repair, deployment, or credible claim of resistance to whole-process credential compromise.

## Verification

The exhaustive GF(5) check tested 1,250 forced-prefix equal-output laws, 125 adaptive histories, nine mixed visibility cases and infeasibility when all messages but not their sum are authorized, and four exact memory-tradeoff cases. Lean 4.34.1 verifies the section-plus-kernel decomposition and injectivity of adding fresh corrections, not the full conditional-probability or entropy argument. Exact AGY/Neon was not available in the local workspace; no substitute, external sharing, PR or deployment.

An independent source audit reviewed the causal lifting and minimax argument under these assumptions and reran the controls. This is not a proof of novelty or a formalization of the full entropy/probability result.
