# Independent audit: scalar repair and availability

**Green for the fixed-view ideal model.** Exact sharp formulas do not give full-input simulation or robust, deployed repair. Explicitly keep declaration/exposure selection exogenous to the secret and hidden state (or prove a separate causal selection result); choosing before the pad coins alone does not guarantee this.

- For fixed eligible H and C the weighted helper map is a linear bijection (exactly t+1 distinct sites, all target Lagrange weights nonzero). Given a secret, uniform masks induce the uniform affine slice in v-coordinates. Once known incident pads are fixed, the difference subspace in v that can preserve the transcript is exactly the incidence image of H\C: exposed coordinates vanish and the remaining pad changes cancel exactly the proposed differences. The repaired sum is always preserved on that image. If it has an edge then the two scalar weights differ by injectivity of C(1−a/x), so every scalar shift is achieved. If no edge exists all pad values relevant to the batch are known and the complete v is determined. The argument requires uniform independent one-use pads and uniform independent initial masks, not a chosen/nonuniform coefficient law.
- With one extra non-helper read, preserving that read adds β=0 on the shift. Exact privacy means α is nonzero on the kernel of β within the incidence image, equivalently α is not in the span of β restricted to that image (including the empty-edge case, where privacy fails). The Lagrange formula for β is C_b(a−x)/(b−x), and each edge has a nonzero β difference since a≠b and endpoints differ. The claimed ratio is a nonzero common coefficient times ((b−u)(b−v))/(uv). Two edge ratios suffice even if both edges share a vertex: their incidence vectors are then independent, and simultaneous pad shifts are allowed. A length-two path ensures distinct ratios because (b−x)/x is injective for b≠0. The result is a specified-H,C,b criterion, not a solved universal graph optimization for extra reads, and does not survive arbitrary further reads.
- For h=k−c≥2, quantifying all committees and all exposures permits every h-subset as the unexposed set. An edge in each h-subset is exactly α(G)<h; the complement is K_h-free, so the balanced-clique number is precisely Turán's complement bound. For full honest-input simulation, incidence image equals the subspace with zero component sums; all-H,C full simulation therefore requires connectivity on every h-subset, equivalent to κ≥n−h+1. The sharp edge bound ceil(nκ/2) is Harary's and includes h=2 and h=n. The 9 versus 42 example is correct under these different privacy objectives. The quantity is pair relations provisioned before readiness, not per-repair active mask count, online communication, or postcommit dropout tolerance.
- Conditioning on fixed H,C and non-helper coordinate b is essential to the affine law. Public readiness/metadata and selection must not themselves leak the secret or alter the conditional distribution; the universal quantifiers can be read as testing independently fixed choices. No private channels are constructed and no physical memory isolation, active misbehavior, late dropout, or simultaneous multi-round guarantee follows.

Primary comparisons: [Bell et al.](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/704) already replace the full pair graph with sparse topologies for stronger aggregation privacy and dropout/collusion settings; [Zhao–Sun](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07750) optimize robust two-round communication, not fixed provisioning in the scalar Shamir objective; [Huang–Bruck](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00500) addresses secure repair and bandwidth. Turán/Harary and pair cancellation are prior; the scalar one-edge criterion and extra-read ratio specialization are the narrow contribution. No exact identical primary result found in the checked sources; no priority assurance.

Reran exact controls: 10,560 field cases and all 32,768 six-vertex graphs passed. Lean checks the three algebraic handles with standard axioms, not the full probability/extremal/availability theorem. No real system was tested.
