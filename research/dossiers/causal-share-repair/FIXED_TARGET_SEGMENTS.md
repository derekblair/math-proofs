# Exact additive refresh budgets for prescribed visible repair targets

Fixed schedule only. This is a scalar Shamir privacy and entropy result under a passive ideal exposure model. It is not a new KEM, end-to-end threshold decapsulation, an adaptive-target privacy theorem, or a deployment.

## Sharp fixed-schedule refresh and joint budgets

Let E≥1 and t≥1. Work over any finite field with the required distinct nonzero share points; for a real interpolation repair at one missing site there are at least t+2 sites, of which at least t+1 are live at repair time. Degree-at-most-t Shamir shares have an unchanged scalar secret s as constant term and an initially uniform independent t-vector of nonconstant coefficients. One predetermined nonzero repair target a_e is announced and its value f_e(a_e) is public in epoch e. The entire target schedule is fixed and known to the designer at setup. There are no additional true-share snapshot reads, refresh-time state or message reads, hidden-buffer observations, or runtime alterations. The only permitted state transformation at seam j between epochs j and j+1 adds an independent fresh uniform sample from a fixed public subspace U_j of degree-at-most-t zero-constant polynomials. Its field-coin cost is d_j=dim U_j (one can ignore excess unused coins). The U_j need not be generic, but are fixed independently of all true shares and observed values.

Partition the ordered target list into nonempty contiguous segments, each containing at most t **distinct** target sites; let m be the minimum possible segment count. Then:

1. The minimum number of fresh independent field elements for perfectly private public target values is exactly m−1. No large-field qualifier is needed beyond valid nonzero points.
2. Greedily take the longest remaining feasible prefix at each step; those boundaries are optimal. Every chosen seam uses exactly one fresh coefficient.
3. If full weighted helper messages are also visible and the repair obeys the separate independently additive causal pad model, the minimum **joint** pad-plus-refresh entropy is t+m−1 field elements. This excludes the initial t Shamir masking elements, which are part of the given state, and counts the pad sampler’s complete private randomness. It is not a bandwidth/communication metric.

## Proof

**Shift characterization.** Flatten the independent initial masks and refresh coefficients into one uniform vector r. Observed scalar values are a linear function Ar+s·1; conditioned on s, each law is uniform on the affine coset im A+s·1. These laws agree for all s exactly when 1 lies in im A; otherwise different s have disjoint supports. Equivalently a unit-secret difference polynomial history g_e exists with g_e(0)=1, g_e(a_e)=0, deg g_e≤t and g_(e+1)−g_e∈U_e. This criterion does not assert the actual difference history is published, or that actual legitimate shares vanish.

**Lower bound.** Whenever U_j=0, the unit-secret difference polynomial cannot change. The nonzero seams therefore cut the epochs into contiguous constant-polynomial segments. In each segment the same nonzero polynomial (constant term 1) vanishes on all its distinct sites, hence has at most t such sites. Thus any private construction uses at least m−1 nonzero seams and spends at least m−1 coins. Equivalently, any interval containing t+1 distinct target sites must contain a nonzero seam.

**Greedy optimum.** Write greedy starts as 1=i_1<i_2<…<i_m. For each j<m, the closed epoch window [i_j,i_(j+1)] contains exactly t+1 distinct targets, because greedy stops just before the first target absent from its full set of t distinct points. Every feasible solution must have an internal seam in this window, inside the half-open seam-index interval [i_j,i_(j+1)). These seam windows are pairwise disjoint even though their epoch windows may share an endpoint. At least m−1 distinct seams are required. Greedy achieves this count. With a hash set the boundary-selection pass is expected linear in E; a balanced set gives deterministic O(E log(t+1)) comparisons. Polynomial coefficient generation is separate.

**Construction.** For each segment with distinct site set S put

    g_S(X) = ∏_(a in S) (1−X/a).

All factors are over the public field. This polynomial has constant 1, degree |S|≤t, and roots exactly S. At the chosen seam from S to T, let U_j=span(g_T−g_S), otherwise U_j=0. The next target a_j+1 is in T and not S, so the seam difference evaluates to −g_S(a_j+1)≠0. Thus its spanning vector is zero-constant and nonzero, and evaluation at the next target has rank exactly one on this space. The sequence of segment witnesses is a unit-secret difference history and proves privacy. All refresh bases are computed from the public schedule before any actual values are known. Actual random increments use independent hidden coefficients along those public directions.

**Joint cost.** For the separate causal additive repair model, a fixed path with t+1 different live weighted helpers per repair has optimal pad cost t+Σ_j (d_j−rank(eval_(a_(j+1))|U_j)). The first repair’s equal-output difference space has dimension t. Each later new kernel direction, holding earlier helper-message coordinates and current target output fixed, lies precisely in U_j∩ker eval_(a_(j+1)). Uniform pad supports can extend chronologically by a section of the prefix projection plus independent uniform new kernel coordinates. The conditional-translation entropy lower bound charges each of those coordinates; see the fixed and adaptive causal pad dossiers. No joint construction in this restricted model can spend below t+m−1: the pads always cost at least t and refresh always costs at least m−1. In the displayed greedy construction every nonzero d_j is 1 and its evaluation rank is 1; other ranks and dims are 0. Thus no new independent pad coin is needed after the initial t, attaining the bound. The pads are independent of the actual secret and refresh coins, with protected state and private pad distribution as in that separate model. The repaired target receives only the weighted masked contributions and adds them, with no target-side pad seed. This does not prove the same lower bound for unrestricted jointly dependent refresh and repair randomization.

## Examples and boundaries

- Degree 2, prescribed targets 1,2,1,3,2,4: greedy segments (1,2,1), (3,2), (4), so exact refresh cost 2 and joint cost 4 over any field with these distinct nonzero sites and enough live helpers. Alternating just 1 and 2 for any number of epochs needs no refresh in this model. A single highly visible trajectory of repairs can consume fewer coins than a schedule required to hide every possible trajectory.
- Predetermined batches of additional visible evaluations: the same refresh-only proof works with a set S_e of targets at each epoch by requiring the union of sites per segment to have size at most t. If some single epoch has more than t distinct evaluations, privacy is impossible. Greedy maximal segments again minimize unit-cost seams. At a boundary at least one site of the next batch falls outside the old roots, giving rank one on evaluation at the new batch. This is not the setting of ordinary adversarially selected snapshots; pad savings also require the next batch to be disclosed at the stage at which its constraints are used.
- Fixed linear observations more generally: a segment is feasible iff there exists a common degree-at-most-t polynomial with constant 1 annihilated by all prescribed linear observations in that segment. Feasibility persists on subsegments, so the same greedy interval witness proof and single seam direction work whenever all singleton segments are feasible. At a seam an old witness cannot annihilate the next observation batch, by maximality, and the new one does. This extension may require solving linear systems to choose segments and does not give the evaluation case’s set-based scan.
- The theorem’s independent-additive restriction is essential. In an unrestricted ideal model even zero fresh coins can suffice for one prescribed moving target: map f_1 to f_e(X)=f_1((a_1/a_e)X). It preserves the secret and degree, and all public values then equal f_1(a_1). This state-dependent coefficient transform is not a fresh independent additive update. It is not a distributed construction or a guarantee under extra reads or adaptive target choices. No absolute refresh lower bound is claimed outside the stated update class.
- Target 0 is excluded because publicly revealing f_e(0) would disclose s. The visible traffic simulation does not itself protect a trajectory that discloses too much. Real active integrity, secure transport and sampler state, and erasure remain separate, along with any application to ML-KEM.

## Verification and primary context

[Herzberg et al., Proactive Secret Sharing](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs754/2001fa/339.PDF), [Huang–Bruck, Generic Secure Repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00500), and [Proactive Secret Sharing with Constant Communication](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/942) are primary context: proactive refresh, repair and communication/robustness address different general objectives. Root-counting, interpolation, greedy contiguous partitioning and the padding machinery are not individually new. This candidate joins them to give an exact fixed-known trajectory entropy formula and a joint optimum in this restricted additive model; a targeted search found no exact same formulation but priority is unestablished.

The synthetic checker exhausts target words of length 1–6 over four nonzero sites and degrees 1–3, compares to all cuts, verifies exact root sets, every seam's nonzero evaluation, and the augmented-rank privacy criterion over GF(7). Lean checks the polynomial roots and rank-one seam witness and the disjoint greedy deficit lower bound; the full finite-field privacy and entropy reduction are proof text, not formally certified. Exact AGY tooling was unavailable; no replacement model, unpublished sharing, remote changes or PR.
