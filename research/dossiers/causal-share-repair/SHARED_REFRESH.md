# Exact correlated repair padding with known cumulative refresh

A separate, stronger-trust model than independent repair padding. No KEM, active verification, credential security, private-channel construction or deployment claim.

## Model and trust change

For E≥1, t≥1, a degree-at-most-t Shamir polynomial at epoch e is f_e=f_1+R_e. The secret is the constant of f_1, unchanged throughout; R_1=0 and each R_e has constant zero. The actual initial mask coefficients, refresh inputs, and true values remain hidden from the transcript observer except the expressly authorized readings. Standard refresh chooses independent uniform increments at public zero-constant directions; write d_j for the dimensions and D=Σ_j d_j. Repair operations restore shares without changing f_e. Refresh inputs and initial polynomial are fixed by an exogenous sharing process, independently of the observer selecting repair targets. Every share site and target is nonzero. At each repair t+1 distinct live helpers excluding the target are available.

The difference from the independent-pad theorem is that a protected sampler now receives **each exact cumulative polynomial R_e by the corresponding repair**, through authenticated/confidential provisioning, or maintains it from the protected increments. It does not know f_1, any real Shamir share, or the actual repaired value. It need not know future targets or refreshes. The sampler has protected persistent storage for its fake polynomial and cumulative refresh (or equivalent inputs), and its input interface is blind to the weighted repair transcript and ordinary snapshots. This separation matters: the same component holding the pad state and observing a full masked repair batch could combine them to recover the underlying polynomial. We do not claim an implementation of that separation. It privately sends only individual pad components to helper slots; the repaired holder receives no pad seed and simply sums the weighted masked messages. Helpers and sampler must erase obsolete working data. Logical share snapshots and the visible repair traffic are not allowed to inspect any such protected state, refresh delivery, private pad links, or helper/recipient buffers. Exposing sampler internals would be a different threat model; refreshing while reliably providing exact inputs to the sampler also needs a protected handoff with no stale state or integrity failure.

A read-only observer can choose a nonzero target and legal helper order using earlier visible messages and authorized values, but not inspect hidden state or future values. The authorized output for each repair is the target share, intrinsically visible by summation if all weighted helper messages are visible. Ordinary true-share reads can be supplied to both real and simulated experiments as the same designated fixed-history value oracle. The result models classical traffic; it says nothing about coherent access to protected memory.

## Exact one-seed construction

At setup sample one uniform degree-at-most-t, zero-constant polynomial F independently from the whole true history and observer policy tape. This costs t field elements. At round e with target a, set

    P_e(X) = (F−R_e)(X) − (F−R_e)(a).

The sampler evaluates P_e at each helper and weights it by that helper’s Lagrange weight for a. Their additive pads sum to P_e(a)=0. A pad polynomial need not have zero constant; its defining property is zero evaluation at the **repair target**, and it never changes a true share. The masked helper messages are the weighted coordinates of

    f_e+P_e
      = y_e + (f_1+F) − (f_1+F)(a),
    where y_e=f_e(a).

Let Z=f_1−f_1(0)+F. For each fixed complete true history, Z is uniform on all zero-constant degree-at-most-t polynomials, by translation of F in that vector space. The masked polynomial is y_e+Z−Z(a). Thus a simulator with only the legitimate repaired value y_e and the current action can sample Z once, compute the same weighted messages, and reuse Z across rounds. It never needs the true cumulative refresh or a future action or output.

**Adaptive validity.** Fix the complete true history h (including actual R), and fix the observer’s private tape. Couple the real run to the simulator using Z=f_1−f_1(0)+F; the simulator’s seed is uniform conditional on h. The two executions produce identical messages for the same past, so the policy chooses the same legal action next; authorized outputs then match from h. This coupling holds every round by induction. Equivalently, for candidate path σ, authorized values y, and traffic m, adaptive probability is forced-path traffic probability multiplied by the indicators for policy compatibility and for y equal to the path’s actual authorized values. An incompatible path may have positive forced-path mass, but contributes zero adaptive mass. No claim of seed uniformity after conditioning on outcome-selected paths is required. Arbitrary postprocessing or observing a subset of otherwise authorized messages preserves this equality; a changed hidden-output guarantee does not automatically follow.

## Matching conditional entropy lower bound

The comparison class permits any (possibly nonuniform) sampler learning R but not true f_1 or secret-dependent data except public action choices, with the full visible helper batch in round one. Require **pointwise** causal simulation for every valid fixed true history, including fixed complete R. The first action must be fixed or forcible regardless of observed hidden data, and conditional on every allowed R, f_1's polynomial space must permit all degree≤t coefficient vectors when comparing secret worlds. The initial t masks and refresh choices are independent; prior inputs may not reduce the allowed coefficient space. On a forced first branch, the joint law of first pad P_1 conditional on R is independent of actual f_1, even if shared setup randomness allows statistical dependence between sampler inputs and R. Target, helper set and weights at the first branch are fixed.

Fix a possible R and two initial polynomials with equal target evaluation. Their difference ranges over the entire t-dimensional kernel of that evaluation. All t+1 helper weights are nonzero, so weighted evaluation gives an invertible coordinate representation and maps this kernel onto the whole sum-zero space of allowed first-batch pads. Pointwise indistinguishability forces the conditional law of P_1 to be invariant under every translation in that space. Since it is supported there by correctness, that law is uniform and H(P_1|R)=t log_2(q); without exact correctness the support premise would have to change. Hence

    H(R,P_1,...,P_E) ≥ H(R)+t log_2(q)

on a forced path. Any charged private random source producing these data has entropy at least this by deterministic data processing; initial Shamir masking entropy is not charged to the sampler and cannot be used as an unmodeled extra sampler input. For independent uniform refresh subspace increments, H(R)=D log_2(q) because cumulative and incremental coordinates are in bijection. The construction pays D+t independent coins, with t pad coins once and no further repair pads drawn. On a forced path its complete pad sequence is deterministic given R and F, and the first pad identifies F via its nonconstant coefficients, so the joint entropy bound is attained exactly.

The lower is **not** a conclusion from ordinary secret privacy only after averaging over the realized refresh. It depends on pointwise simulation with fixed R and no side information or prior constraint coupling actual f_1 to the sampler at the first round. It is a worst/forced path statement if actions are adaptive, not entropy conditional on a selected path. Likewise the construction's distribution equality does not make a trajectory of authorized true shares private if that trajectory already determines the secret. If an observer learns R itself, the simulation statement can still hold with that extra public side information for fixed h; the underlying secret can nevertheless become determinable from the now-debiased authorized outputs, and no claim under leakage of refresh is made.

## Exact stationary-target joint optimum and scope

For publicly visible repairs at one fixed nonzero point a, with b_e additional allowed ordinary snapshot reads away from a in epoch e, the earlier reduced-degree interval criterion gives the sharp sufficiently-large-field least boundary cost D_min. Conditioning on the actual target outputs removes one initial dimension and each exposed evaluation dimension per boundary; choosing increments in ker eval_a leaves t−1 effective degree and wastes no boundary dimensions. Thus D_min is the minimum Σ d_j satisfying Σ_(e in I)b_e ≤ t−1+Σ_(internal j in I)d_j for each contiguous epoch interval (feasible single worlds require b_e≤t−1). This is a refresh privacy statement, independent of padding, so extra trust in the pad sampler does not reduce D_min for the given additive model. Combined with the current theorem, **joint refresh plus pad optimum is t+D_min, versus t+2D_min in the independently randomized pad model**. Example t=5, six epochs, one extra read per epoch: D_min=2, controlled by the whole six-epoch interval and attained by two dimensions at the middle boundary over a sufficiently large field. Shared-refresh total 7; independent optimal total 9; independent fresh pad per round comparator total 32. None of these reduces bandwidth or verifies a distributed realization. For the predetermined and zero-extra-read segment theorem, optimal dimensions are already one with evaluation rank one at each seam, so this trust change need not save any coins.

A narrow generalization is algebraic: if a public linear output map L on the polynomial has a public linear section J for its image, use P=(id−J L)(F−R), which satisfies LP=0 and produces masked polynomial J(L f)+(id−J L)(f_1+F). The same t-seed simulation follows if the projection removes the constant contribution, i.e. (id−J L) applied to constant polynomials is zero. Scalar evaluation with J the constant embedding satisfies it. A mixed-visibility setting can always take a fixed restriction of the full authorized transcript. But the t lower bound need not hold with fewer visible coordinates, and neither arbitrary linear outputs, changing raw visibility disclosed retroactively, withheld repair sums, nor mixed fields inherits a sharp t optimum from this. Active robustness, endpoint compromise, protection of R in transport, and private decapsulation are outside this theorem.

Primary comparator: [Huang–Bruck secure repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00500) studies private repair communication and bandwidth; [Herzberg et al.](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs754/2001fa/339.PDF) introduce classical proactive refreshing; [Proactive Secret Sharing with Constant Communication](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/942) is relevant to modern mobile corruption and protected distribution. One-time masking and affine translation are standard. This candidate is the exact conditional joint cost and the simple online cancel-refresh construction under the specific extra trust assumption; no priority claim.
