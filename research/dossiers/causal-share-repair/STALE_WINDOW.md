# Exact stale-share cost and a conditional ML-KEM custody reduction

Defensive, ideal read-only theory. The result does not provide threshold ML-KEM, forward-secure encryption, hardware erasure, private channels, or a deployable oracle. No target, actual keys or ciphertexts were tested.

## A sharp unavoidable retention window

Let E≥1, field F_q, and n>t distinct nonzero custodian points. Degree-at-most-t Shamir shares protect one unchanged scalar s. Initial nonconstant coefficients are uniform. Each boundary adds a private independent uniform sample of a public zero-constant linear subspace; fully independent resharing is allowed. A read at a custodian in epoch e yields its current share and the most recent L predecessor-epoch shares there, when they exist. This represents an ideal logical stale-slot exposure: no retained protocol traffic, local buffers, private refresh channels, present operation state, active writes or additional metadata-dependent leakage. It does not model full process or device exposure. An observer may select b_e distinct sites per epoch, with each b_e≤n.

With arbitrary independent refresh resources, perfect scalar privacy against every bounded fixed view is possible **iff**

    min(n, Σ_(e=j)^(min(E−1,j+L)) b_e) ≤ t for every origin epoch j.

Necessity: origin polynomial f_j appears on each of those epochs' snapshots at the chosen sites. The sets can cover min(n,sum b_e) distinct points; if ≥t+1, their evaluations uniquely determine the origin polynomial regardless of refresh. Sufficiency: add independent full-degree masks at every boundary so that all epoch polynomials have independent uniform nonconstant coefficients given s. Every origin polynomial is then seen at ≤t sites in each fixed view; by interpolation these values are uniform and secret-independent, independently across origin epochs. Adaptive site selection follows by the fixed-transcript leaf argument: hold the observer policy tape fixed, and compare probabilities of each transcript branch using its forced list of reads. The bound is exactly about how long immutable versions remain visible, not ideal time on a calendar. For a constant per-epoch budget b>0 and sufficient elapsed epochs, L must satisfy (L+1)b≤t; faster refresh alone cannot compensate for stale versions held this long.

## Exact independent refresh entropy with one stale slot and three epochs

Index current epochs 0,1,2. Each epoch-0 snapshot returns only f_0 at its sites; epochs 1 and 2 return both current and immediately preceding shares at the same chosen sites S_1 and S_2, of sizes at most b_1 and b_2. At epoch 0 the budget is b_0. Write Δ_0=f_1−f_0 and Δ_1=f_2−f_1. They are independent uniform polynomials in public zero-constant subspaces U_0,U_1 with dimensions d_0,d_1. The model has no extra initial version before epoch 0. No refresh direction is chosen in response to the read set. Well-defined designs take all b_i≤n.

**Theorem.**
- If b_0+b_1>t or b_1+b_2>t, privacy is impossible for any such refresh (and even beyond the additive scheme): one origin polynomial is exposed at more than t points.
- Otherwise let λ=max(0,b_0+b_1+b_2−t). If λ=0, d_0=d_1=0 suffices over every field.
- If λ>0, necessarily d_0≥b_1+λ regardless of d_1. Over every sufficiently large field there is a fixed public U_0 attaining equality, and d_1=0 is enough. The first and last seams are not interchangeable: last-seam randomness never helps against the stipulated three-epoch view.

**Exact reduction of the observed data.** For each stale/current pair (u,v), replace it by (u,v−u), a reversible coordinate change. The whole view is equivalent to

    f_0 on S_0∪S_1;   f_1 on S_2;   Δ_0 on S_1;   Δ_1 on S_2.

The last observation is an independent sample function with no secret information. Crucially it cannot hide information in f_1: the stale share in the same snapshot is already present. This proves d_1 irrelevance for a fixed view independent of all other claims. If λ=0 take an unchanged polynomial, whose overall view has at most t distinct sites.

Set C=S_1 and take |C|=b_1 (smaller views can be padded, choosing C and additional reads separately). Condition on f_0(C) and Δ_0(C). Their joint law is secret-independent: first f_0(C) uniform for |C|≤t, second independent of s and f_0. Subtract public fixed zero-constant representatives of those known values. Divide the remaining two polynomials and private refresh directions by G_C(X)=∏_(x in C)(1−X/x), which has constant 1 and is nonzero at other sites. This gives a degree-m=t−b_1 Shamir polynomial with unchanged constant s and uniform initial masks, and an effective hidden refresh of dimension

    u_C=d_0−rank(Eval_C restricted to U_0),

uniform on a subspace of zero-constant degree-m polynomials after division. Observations outside C become up to b_0 and b_2 ordinary reads in its two epochs, up to known invertible scale/translation. Every allowed choice of C is possible after the public U_0 is chosen.

**Lower bound for λ>0.** In this reduced problem choose b_0 sites for the first reads (b_0≤m). A unit-secret shift preserving those readings lies in an affine set of dimension m−b_0. After effective refresh the next candidate polynomial has affine dimension at most m−b_0+u_C. Applying second-epoch point equations greedily makes it inconsistent within b_2 equations if u_C<λ. To see there is always a new effective equation while consistent, choose a member of the affine set; its degree ≤m polynomial with constant 1 cannot vanish on all n−b_1>m sites, whereas all previously selected equations vanish. Since the global U_0 evaluates injectively as polynomial functions on all n sites, those evaluation functionals span U_0's dual. Choose C including min(d_0,b_1) independent such site rows, then pad C to size b_1. Consequently some C has u_C=d_0−min(d_0,b_1). Requiring u_C≥λ>0 forces d_0≥b_1+λ. This argument is valid for every eligible field.

**Upper bound for sufficiently large fields.** Choose b_1+λ independent uniformly sampled candidate coefficient columns in the degree-t zero-constant space as a public setup experiment (conditional on finding a valid basis, not refreshed per request). For a fixed C the first b_1 columns project to independent uniform vectors on F_q^b1, spanning it except with probability <1/(q−1). Conditional on those columns, subtract their unique measured components from the remaining λ columns. The latter project to independent uniform directions in ker Eval_C. For any fixed two sets of outside reads A,B in the reduced degree-m sharing: if |A∪B|≤m, a constant-one polynomial vanishing on all sites already gives the needed secret shift. Otherwise their initial coefficient rows span all m directions, leaving a λ-dimensional quotient of the observations (for maximal cardinalities). The refresh evaluation on B surjects onto that quotient because initial directions can realize any values on A and refresh directions can realize any values on B. The λ random columns span the quotient except with probability <1/(q−1). Counting fixed C,A,B and applying a union bound suffices, for example if

    q−1 > binom(n,b_1) · [1+binom(n−b_1,b_0)binom(n−b_1,b_2)].

In the critical λ>0 case at least one permitted view with |A∪B|>m exists, so the successfully sampled total d_0 columns are independent (success on that quotient plus surjective initial exposed part certifies it). Publish the validated basis; only independent scalar coefficients used with it at the real boundary count as refresh coins. This is an existence bound, not an efficient construction claim. No identity about refreshed full process states follows.

**Comparison with prompt erasure.** With current-only snapshots and the same feasible b_0+b_1≤t, b_1+b_2≤t constraints, the optimal total refresh cost over sufficiently large fields is λ. For a fixed refreshed input view, if Σ b>t a greedy sequence of point constraints on the unit-secret affine history of dimension ≤t+Σ d establishes the matching lower bound. For the upper, refresh at one seam separating group S_0 from S_1∪S_2, which have respective read caps b_0 and b_1+b_2≤t, and apply the same elementary two-epoch quotient proof with no standing C. Thus the one-slot stale window costs exactly b_1 additional field coins when λ>0, and can impose an impossibility threshold in other budgets. Example: degree two, b_0=b_1=b_2=1, four physical sites, GF(5): clean storage costs one field coin, stale storage costs two at the first seam. The sharp E=3 economy says nothing about a full optimal entropy profile for arbitrary horizons; fresh full resharing gives the separate retention criterion above.

## Conditional composition with a standard PQ KEM

[FIPS 203](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.203.pdf) Alg. 16 and §7.1 specify the two 32-byte seeds (d,z) and permit sensitive seed storage for deterministic key expansion. Both matter: z is part of implicit rejection. Encode the full seed injectively in one large field (e.g. a fixed binary linear bijection with F_(2^512)), or use independent masks for separately shared components. This is only a representation of at-rest secret shares, not the field used for K-PKE arithmetic or evidence of useful MPC conversion speed. Quantify refresh costs in field elements of the chosen representation, with initial masks and all ideal setup separately charged.

Consider a hybrid service that uses exactly standard KeyGen, Encaps and Decaps and keeps no interface-visible state except (a) authorized standard-game outputs from an ideal evaluation oracle and (b) declared snapshots of logical shares. Require an actual candidate implementation, **before** inheriting this hybrid proof, to simulate all observable protocol outputs, scheduling metadata and session traces from that functionality and those exact snapshots, even with dynamically chosen holders and no recoverable old private messages. In a deployed service plaintext/session keys are not returned to a general requester; authenticated application-scoped authorization or secret-shared output is a separate constraint. The IND-CCA game only gives the appropriate ideal challenger interface with challenged ciphertext barred. No raw invalidity/rejection bit, callback, candidate or fallback secret, or independent widened decapsulation interface is introduced by the reduction.

**Composition lemma.** If every forced bounded snapshot list has a secret-independent law as above, then in the hybrid service there is zero additional secret information from the snapshots, even when a read or request is selected adaptively from previous authorized operation results. A simulator need not know the true seed. Sample a fake logical share history around any fixed dummy seed with the same public bases, answer all snapshot queries from it, and forward authorized standard-game queries unchanged. Fix the true seed, functionality random tape (including challenge values), and adversary's classical action-control tape. A candidate sequence of commands and observations has probability given by the forced snapshot law times indicators for compatible decisions and for the prescribed outputs of the functionality. Forced snapshot equality gives exactly the same probability with the dummy history. The same argument uses a classical-interface decision instrument for quantum adversaries: condition on the classical transcript; their private state evolution and next-message distributions depend only on that transcript, not the hidden sharing tape. No coherent share or decapsulation query model is covered. If a candidate implementation has a separately established composable realization error ε for this ideal service, its distinguishing advantage is bounded by the underlying standard-game advantage plus ε (for a single-bit probability-difference convention). No such real realization is supplied, so this is not a security claim about an implementation or an upgrade to the KEM itself.

**What the window never means.** Refresh preserves the same seed and published key. A later full seed disclosure compromises past standard ciphertexts for that key, regardless of how cleanly old shares were erased: standard deterministic decapsulation can still use that seed. Window protection here means bounded logical-share observations across time, not forward secrecy after a later full compromise. Achieving the latter requires a separate standard-key rotation/forward-secure envelope design with different actual decryption capabilities; not achieved by replacing share masks.

## Primary prior art and limitations

- [Proactive Secret Sharing with Constant Communication](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/942.pdf), Appendix C.5 already notes that an adversary during refresh can learn both old and new shares, plus additional state. [Proactive Secret Sharing without Erasures](https://opencryptology.org/2026/1072) establishes an important stronger no-erasures context; the classical unbounded-history obstruction is not new. [On the (Im)possibility of Asynchronous Proactive Secret Sharing](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2018) studies different asynchronous corruption-window models. The exact three-epoch logical cache cost, last-seam irrelevance and zero-extra-loss hybrid separation are the narrow candidate here; limited search, no absolute priority.
- [NIST IR 8214C](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2026/NIST.IR.8214C.pdf), §§9.2.4, 10.2 and A.2.2 distinguishes active/adaptive/proactive analysis from functional equivalence and notes full thresholdization costs of standard ML-KEM. [Ta–Liu–Chau–Wang](https://opencryptology.org/2026/2220) propose exact-compatible semi-honest MPC and report round counts; their benchmark is not improved by layering an abstract custody result. Active consistency, real refresh, adaptive state exposure, authorization, session outputs and time-dependent key erasure are all still open here.
