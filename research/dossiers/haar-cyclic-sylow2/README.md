# Power-of-two cycles for cubic Haar graphs with cyclic Sylow 2-subgroups

The attached paper proof covers every finite connected simple cubic Haar graph over a group whose Sylow 2-subgroups are cyclic, with no bound on the size of the cyclic 2-part. The same conclusion holds after retaining any three cross-orbit ports in a simple bi-Cayley graph over such a group. An odd group is allowed (the Sylow 2-subgroup is then trivial). See **Proof.md** for the precise statement, companion two-rail cases, and the limits of the result.

- Related primary prior work: [Burkhart, Odd-ordered Cayley graphs satisfy Erdős–Gyárfás](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05060) is a one-orbit Cayley theorem, and [Ghaffari–Mostaghim](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00010-017-0518-3) supplies the dihedral Cayley case. No priority claim is made for the Haar statement. The unrestricted conjecture remains open.
