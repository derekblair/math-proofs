# Archival witness for the known bound R(3,14) ≥ 67

Kolodyazhny's original Aluarium article reports a triangle-free graph of order 66 with independence number 13. The original domain was not reachable in our public checks. The identical square binary matrix is reproduced by its [2025 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20250220001417id_/http://aluarium.net/forum/wiki-article-17.html) and [2022 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20220121030544id_/http://aluarium.net/forum/wiki-article-17.html). These are the data cited as Kol2 by [Radziszowski, *Small Ramsey Numbers*, revision 18](https://www.cs.rit.edu/~spr/ElJC/sur.pdf). This work verifies a known result; it does not improve the Ramsey bound or claim mathematical priority.

`archive66-adjacency.txt` is the 0/1 adjacency matrix from the archived article in its original order. Columns are indexed from zero in Lean and encoded least-significant bit first. The two raw HTTP body SHA-256 fingerprints, respectively, are `4951bea75dde05b2fcccff1b87b23a73cb1549904991d89e7c820a620083cb17` and `dade269981a6887a1726940425be9bd76dabe9439db44c9a9f4318f4cdfedc00`. Independent HTML parsing matched both matrices and all Lean masks. A separate complement clique search checked that alpha is 13, and direct checking found 420 edges and no triangles.

`Archive66Endpoint.lean` derives `ramsey314_ge67` from the explicit graph. The independence upper bound uses a finite 14,421-node branching certificate. Each test is checked by ordinary Lean kernel reduction or combined using the checked search theorem. This does not use native_decide or custom axioms. `Archive66AllK.lean` feeds this witness into the already established clique-transversal implication and covers all possible H at orders 63–66 in the K₄-free setting. The [Ramsey-41](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v32i4p30) and [Hall classification](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.3190040206) inputs remain explicit and are not replayed here. This does not address order 62 or the general conjecture.

Replay with Lean 4.34.1 and Mathlib v4.34.1 (d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612). Put the compiled dependencies of `RamseySearch` and `N64to66` plus the Mathlib library paths on LEAN_PATH, and compile the new files in `archive66-order.txt` order. The final kernel reports only propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. `archive66-sha256.txt` binds the replay sources and data.
