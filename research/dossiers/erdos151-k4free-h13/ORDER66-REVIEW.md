It finds one transversal of size ≤n−k meeting every inclusion-maximal clique of size at least two, not ordinary edge clique cover or the different Erdős–Gallai–Tuza triangle packing/deletion conjecture. Taking k=H(n) gives the desired finite-order slice; no assertion that H(n)=13, nor that arbitrary K4-containing graphs are covered.
