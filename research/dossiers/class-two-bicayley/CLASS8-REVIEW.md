# Independent scope and certificate audit: class-eight ribbon

**Green for the reported theorem conditional only on its stated group assumptions.** This audit inspects sources and the pinned theorem logs; it is not a fresh independent whole-project replay. Root is replaying the imported certificate chain.

- The statement in Ribbon8Full.lean is for finite G, lower central term LS G 8 = bottom (nilpotency class at most 8), odd cardinality not divisible by 3 (equivalently order coprime to 6), and nonidentity ports x,y. It gives a simple cycle of length 4,8,16,32,64 or 128 in the ribbon graph. It does **not** cover all odd-order groups, even orders, all bi-Cayley graphs, or general Erdős–Gyárfás. It does not require x≠y or that the original pair generate G. No further hypothesis of order prime to 5 or 7 is required in Ribbon8Full (unlike the older intermediate Ribbon8Family theorem).
- Same-rim geometry is actual: Ribbon5Bridge's chart/word interprets ports 0/1 as inverse directions in the current rim (x on false, y on true), and port 2 as the identity side-matching. The graph witness is an injective finite sequence with cyclic wrap, not just an identity word or a cycle in its class-two image. Quotient separation pulls back noncoincidence of true vertices; collisions not detected by the low quotient are handled by checked conjugacy identities.
- Restricting to the subgroup generated by x,y legitimately inherits LS8=bottom by monotonicity and the order conditions by cardinal divisibility; the subtype lifts adjacency and injection. For the resulting generating pair, if the projected commutator is trivial in G/LS2 the projected generators commute, so LS1≤LS2. Antitonicity gives equality and applying commutator with top propagates it to LS8=bottom. In particular the ports commute and the class-two lemma covers them.
- Otherwise the order of projected C divides the ambient subgroup order. If C^k=1 for 1≤k≤7, its order divides k, hence (by oddness) is 1,3,5,7. The first contradicts nonidentity, 3 is excluded by the explicit order hypothesis, and 5/7 go to Ribbon8Small.projected_order_5_or_7. This is a case split on a single element in the quotient; it does not assume a p-group or remove every p-primary component. All other orders meet the local chart's small-power guard.
- In the exact 5/7 lane, kernel tables check the actual words and all pair cases (32-prefix low; 64-prefix low or R-mate; full chart low or listed conjugate mate). Algebra gives closure for the prefix if R=1, the next if Z=1, and the full word under nilpotency. The listed mate targets are R±, Z± and two ratios; if R,Z are nontrivial and equal, the stored Z conjugate of [U,R] and commute(Z,U) would force Z=1, so ratios are also nontrivial. If no target cycles exist, each of the three alternative charts yields an actual low-collision with zero i,j displacement and |c displacement|=5 or 7, as verified by the order-specific collision tests. Mod LS3 the group is class≤3 and C^p is central; the three relations isolate V and a power of U coprime to its p-torsion. Thus U,V lie in LS3 in the original group. The generating-pair quotient argument then gives LS2=LS3, hence LS2=bottom by nilpotency, and a class-two cycle. Neither a random chart search nor the empirical UT witness is trusted by this proof.
- Existing pinned logs report only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound for Ribbon8.odd_not_three_class8, Ribbon8Small.absence_forces and Ribbon8Small.projected_order_5_or_7. Independent native rerun of check_class8_ut9 reports 272 exact parameter pairs and, for appended (0,1), a simple C128 with a nonzero eighth iterated commutator (entry (0,8) = 8). This is a positive finite arithmetic control, not a formal sharpness proof: it excludes none of lengths 4,8,16,32,64, hence cannot show 128 is necessary. Ambient UT9(F17) need not be generated by the chosen pair; the checked eighth commutator does show the generated subgroup has class at least eight, consistent with the standard ambient degree filtration. No witness minimum-order or priority claim follows.
