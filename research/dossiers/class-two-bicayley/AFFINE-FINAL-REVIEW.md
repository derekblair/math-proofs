# Independent review: all finite odd-order metabelian ribbons

**Green for the assembled kernel-verified mathematical statement.** This audit traces the named imports and existing exact replay, and independently reran the integer certificate validator. It is not a second whole-dossier rebuild or public priority search.

## Actual theorem and graph

RibbonAffine.finite_odd_metabelian_ribbon in RibbonAffineFinal.lean takes finite G, abelian derived subgroup (IsMulCommutative (commutator G)), odd Nat.card G, and nonidentity x,y. There is no ambient nilpotency, generation, or x≠y premise. The output is a simple cycle of length 4, 8, 16 or 32 in the specific one-matching bi-Cayley graph on Bool × G: same-rim neighbors by right multiplication by x± or y±, plus identity matching. This is not the all-crossing Haar graph or a theorem about all bi-Cayley graphs, nor about even order. The nonidentity ports and oddness make layer neighbors distinct and ensure the graph is cubic.

## Group reduction and bridge

The wrapper first restricts to closure {x,y}; it inherits odd cardinality and metabelianity, and lifts a cycle to G using the *injective* subtype homomorphism. Commuting ports use the class-two routine. Nontrivial projected commutator in G/LS2 uses the separately established neutral metabelian C32 result, including its projected order-three branch. It does not use a nilpotency hypothesis. For the stationary branch portC lies in LS2 but is not 1. Inside the generated subgroup its normal closure is the derived subgroup D, and D=[D,G]. Conjugation acts on abelian D through G/D; a simple quotient as a finite module over Z[G/D] gives a finite field and nonzero scalar-action quotient. The displacement cocycle detects portC, and normalization is valid after possibly swapping the rims. A common exponent n=Nat.card G is odd and annihilates both multipliers; the additive field order divides |D|, excluding characteristic 2. Swapping x,y is only rim symmetry; no extra lifting assumption is introduced.

The callback does not close an image and then assume it lifts. In certified_affine, cycle_from_at is applied directly to pathAt x y in the *source* group, with size_word and close_word as its length/closure witnesses. RibbonAffineWords closes each selected length-32 word by writing its source-group value as a commutator of elements in the derived subgroup (up to conjugation), so metabelianity gives actual closure. position_sound evaluates each original prefix in affine coordinates. Any hypothetical equal original vertices then have equal images, contradicting the selected injective chart. The affine map need not itself be injective globally. Accordingly the final output includes original adjacency, wrap and pairwise distinct vertices.

## Integer and exceptional-field certificates

If the base word has a collision, RibbonAffineBase uses checked pair classifications, primitive exponent relations and Laurent factor identities to reduce it to one of fourteen listed parameterized univariate obstructions. RibbonAffineFinal checks that the branch exponent/polynomial records agree with the corresponding universal table. In a putative affine collision the Laurent translation difference, obstruction polynomial, and multiplier discrepancy all vanish; odd multiplicative order permits stripping the two-power in the last exponent. The checked integer identity then forces the certificate constant to vanish, contradicting the nonzero branch constant (of which that certificate value is a divisor). The integer validator independently reproduced all 3,374 actual frozen identity rows; the Lean theorem checks identities rather than trusting the generator.

The branch constants are 2, 5, 10 or 42. Nonzero two and absence of zero divisors leave only characteristic 5 for 5/10, and 3 or 7 for 42. RibbonAffineModCases covers every bad characteristic/branch pair in the case split. Char 5 and char 3 alternatives shown there contradict the odd exponent via checked modular factorizations; char 7 branches 6/7 reduce to scalar t=2 or t=4 and use separate kernel-checked affine chart 6. This is characteristic in the auxiliary field, not an order restriction on G. In particular finite odd groups divisible by 3, 5 or 7 are not omitted.
