- LS G n means gamma_(n+1); the hypothesis LS G 3 = bottom means class at most three. The selected hypothesis of the principal 32-cycle is U²≠1 or V²≠1, with c=[x^-1,y^-1], U=[c,x], V=[c,y]. The odd corollary can ask only for the selected commutator to be nonidentity because Nat.card being odd implies finiteness and excludes nonidentity involutions. The stronger disjunctive result permits infinite groups with no nonidentity involutions. No generation, global connectedness, or general even-order claim is needed.
- Adj is the simple undirected cubic graph on two copies of G linked by identity matching: first rim moves x±1, second y±1, with both squares nontrivial in the cubic scope. The walk has exactly sixteen rim moves and sixteen matching edges, including the seam. The g-coordinates occupy sixteen distinct labels. A row's layer determines its second visit on the other side; `geometry` proves (side, wrapped group-label index) is injective.

Once the three low exponents agree, the selected third-layer exponent differs by a unit and the other by zero.
