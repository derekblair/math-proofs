- Ribbon.Adj is undirected movement by ±x on the false side, ±y on the true side, plus the identity perfect matching. It is not the all-crossing three-port Haar graph. Under nonidentity ports and involution-free G, the layer neighbors are distinct from one another and the original vertex, so degree is exactly three. No x≠y, generation, or finiteness hypothesis is needed. Cycle n is an injective Fin n vertex map with checked cyclic adjacency.

- Algebra in target_power_from_proj: c^n trivial in G/LS2 implies c^n∈LS2 and its commutator q with x lies in LS3 and is central. Iterating c*x=x*c*(u*a), using c commuting with u*a, yields (u*a)^n=q; central a then makes u^n central. Analogously v^n is central. Iterating the next right-exchange relations forces a^n,b^n,d^n=1. No torsion assumption on the quotient is used.
- Fallback: all a,b,d trivial force u and v to commute with both ports (shared b in the two cross relations). Only in the generated subgroup does this imply central u,v. Mod center, c centralizes the two projected generators, yielding class≤2 there and law LS3=bottom in the generated subgroup. The ambient theorem first restricts to closure {x,y}; lower central series monotonicity and cycle transport by injective subtype are valid.
