# Independent all-odd class-six review

No proof blocker in the stated Lean scope. `Ribbon6.odd_class6` gives a simple C4, C8, C16, C32 or C64 in the single-matching bi-Cayley ribbon for any nonidentity rim pair in a finite odd-order group of nilpotency class at most six. It does not assume group order prime to three, ambient generation, connectedness, or a lower bound on individual generator orders. Cubicity follows since both nonidentity rim elements in the finite odd-order group have order at least three. It is not a theorem for bipartite Haar graphs or all cubic graphs.

The general wrapper restricts to the subgroup generated by the rim pair; subgroup and quotient orders remain odd. The projected commutator being trivial forces abelianness by successive lower-central stabilization. Its having order three activates the new character branch, with a class-two fallback if the two third-layer port commutators vanish and port swap if only the second is nontrivial. Every other nontrivial odd projected order avoids exponents 1–4 and uses the earlier class-six branch.

The class-two projection kills u, so the order of c' there is still three. These are finite **row bounds**, not orders of group elements. The proof only needs actual equality to imply key equality, not the reverse.
