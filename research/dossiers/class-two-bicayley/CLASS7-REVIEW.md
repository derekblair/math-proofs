# Independent class-seven ribbon review

**Verdict:** The checked upper theorem is sound in its stated scope. No sharpness theorem. `Ribbon7.odd_not_three_class7` applies to finite groups with nilpotency class **at most seven**, odd order not divisible by three, and two nonidentity generators for the rims (they need not generate the ambient group). It provides a simple cycle of length 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 or 128 in the single-matching bi-Cayley graph. The cubic assertion uses that a nonidentity element in a finite odd-order group cannot be an involution, making each rim vertex's forward and reverse neighbors distinct. The result is not about every cubic graph, arbitrary Haar graphs, or class-seven groups of odd order divisible by three.

- **Lower central indexing:** `L G n` is γ_(n+1). Put q=[x,y⁻¹], g=x⁻¹[y,x²]x, h=gq⁻². In the class-two quotient g=q², so h lies in γ3. The exact collected expression makes R=UV a conjugate of [q,h]; R is in γ5. U is in γ2 by exact abelianization. Z=[U,V]=U⁻¹[U,R]U is consequently in γ7 and central. More strongly h∈γ4 implies R∈γ6 and Z∈γ8=1. The Lean closure uses these group laws, not Magnus sufficiency.
- **Closures and separations:** If R=1, W32 closes and the low chart separates every pair. If R≠1 and Z=1, W64 closes and its nine projected mates are separated by conjugates of R or R⁻¹Z=R⁻¹. If Z≠1, W128 has group displacement ZK⁻¹Z⁻¹K=1. Its relative differences include R, Z and R⁻¹Z up to conjugacy. R=1 would force Z=1. Likewise R⁻¹Z=1 would make R=Z central, forcing Z=1. Of the 27 projected exceptions, 24 use these free group identities, and precisely (48,98), (49,99), (50,100) use the class-three quotient G/γ4: each relative difference maps to h⁻¹ (up to a conjugacy trivial there). An equality of these vertices would put h in γ4 and force Z=1. I independently parsed the three tables and reproduced the complete exception counts 0, 9, 27. Adjacent moves are the three literal graph ports; pathAt/cycle_from_at enforce closing adjacency and distinct vertices, not just a closed walk.
- **Order conditions:** The low chart only needs the projected commutator to avoid powers 1–4. The code compares projected coordinates by further commutating in the class-two quotient, so it does not require independent abelianized generators. Move to the generated subgroup first; oddness and the 3 exclusion survive divisibility, including in its class-two quotient. If the projected commutator is trivial, the two images generate an abelian quotient, so γ2=γ3, stabilization and nilpotence force γ2=1, and the earlier 4/8/16 result applies.
