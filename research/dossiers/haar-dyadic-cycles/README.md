# A sharp dyadic cycle bound for class-two cubic Haar graphs
Let G be a group and let 1, x, y be three distinct elements.
Suppose xy = yxz, where the commutator z commutes with both x and y.
The bipartite Haar graph on Bool × G, with each left g adjacent to
right g, gx, gy, has a simple cycle of length 4, 8, or 16. The group
can be finite or infinite; only the subgroup generated by x and y is
required to have central commutator. The exact Lean theorem is
[HaarCentral.dyadic_cycle](HaarCentral.lean). The bound 16 is attained
on the Heisenberg group over the field of five elements:
[HaarCentral.example_requires_sixteen](HaarCentral.lean) verifies a 16-cycle
and excludes 4- and 8-cycles in the exact graph. The example also has no
6-cycle, so it is outside the girth-six hypothesis cited below.

For a nontrivial commutator, the proof uses three normal-form cycle
words, separated by conjugation and the order of the commutator. In
the order-two case a collision in the 16-cycle word forces a shorter
two-port cycle. For commuting ports, a lattice 16-cycle either projects
injectively or reveals one of thirteen small exact relations. For each
admissible relation a short closed walk projects injectively, or a
second relation forces a 4-cycle or violates distinctness of the ports.
For sharpness, any simple 4-, 6-, or 8-cycle gives a closed alternating port
word with no immediate repeat. The finite word check excludes these
words for either starting side, and Lean proves the precise link to
injective cyclic adjacency.

## Scope and related work
This is a restricted case of the [Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28594),
not a solution of the full conjecture, or even a result for arbitrary
Haar graphs with noncentral commutator.
[Burkhart](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.05060v1) treats Cayley graphs of
finite nilpotent groups, with a symmetric generator set on one copy
of the group, a different hypothesis than this Haar theorem.
[Gebendorfer](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22666267) proves a dyadic
length bound 32 for cubic bipartite vertex-transitive graphs of girth six,
with an 8/16 result for the toroidal family. The commuting-port analysis
here is a separate formal construction; novelty is not claimed for
the overlapping toroidal conclusion. [Tranquilli](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02675)
has a finite cubic-bipartite size bound. No absolute priority claim,
no absence claim for shorter non-dyadic cycles, and no graph-order bound.

## Reproduction
Use Lean 4.34.1 and mathlib commit
`d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612` with its dependencies built.
Run `bash verify.sh /path/to/mathlib4`. This builds the nine modules
in a fresh temporary directory; no repository build target is changed.
