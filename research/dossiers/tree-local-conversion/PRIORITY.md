# Literature check (November 25, 2026)

- [Zhang, Lv, and Wang, AAMAS 2023](https://www.ifaamas.org/Proceedings/aamas2023/pdfs/p2205.pdf), Definition 2.4, Algorithm 2, Theorem 5.1, and Section 6: the original paper proves optimality of its recursive rule, then explicitly leaves the time complexity on trees and paths open. It predicts intractability on trees and polynomial time on paths.
- Exact-title, author-and-topic, and follow-up searches found the proceedings version, its institutional copies, and related influence models, but no earlier algorithm for the exact tree-local problem. This is a bounded literature check, not proof of absolute priority.
- The new claim is the polynomial, exact evaluation on trees from a node's own full view. Neither the underlying four-point inequality for trees nor the original optimality theorem is presented as new. General-graph complexity and other influence models are not covered.
