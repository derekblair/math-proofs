# Exact chronological quantum privacy for copied share catalogs

Ideal quantum oracle theorem and defensive audit complexity. Not a network protocol, attack procedure, hardware result, standard KEM, or extension of the production threat model in the classical catalog paper.

## Oracle and chronology

All origin Shamir versions share the same scalar s and have jointly independent, uniform nonconstant masks, conditioned on s. Every origin has degree bound t and distinct nonzero labeled evaluations. During phase e, the finite public catalog offers a fixed set of compartment addresses. A compartment is a padded, public-format vector of copied field evaluations, possibly from several origins. Oracle O_e adds that vector to the response register over the field; an explicit zero address adds nothing. Catalogs, lengths, timing and eligibility are independent of secret values. The history is already sampled and does not depend on queries. No access to the underlying storage process, erased or transport buffers not in the catalog, writes, or real decapsulation is assumed.

A chronological **invocation** is one call to the phase oracle in a globally chosen phase, even when its address register contains zero or superposes zero with other addresses. There are caps q_e on calls per phase and Q total. In the first variant the allocation of slots is fixed in advance; in the second an observer may choose later invocation slots and advancement classically using prior measured outputs, always in phase order. In the second variant each schedule decision is a classical/dephased record, so different realized invocation schedules do not interfere. There is no single invocation whose phase is itself a coherent superposition of different times or clocks. This is the ordinary fixed-oracle-invocation accounting in the stated chronological model.

## Exact privacy criterion

Call a set of compartments U a potential pair union when it is the union of the distinct nonzero-address covers of two computation paths sharing the same actual invocation slots. Let m_e be the number of its compartments in phase e. Then U is a potential pair union under some allowable schedule exactly when

    m_e ≤ 2q_e for every phase,        Σ_e ceil(m_e/2) ≤ Q.

The maximum distinct-label counts are taken separately for each origin. Perfect information-theoretic query privacy (all final states identical for every s, against every permitted observer) holds **iff every potential pair union covers at most t distinct labels of each origin**.

**Combinatorics.** If paths share l_e invocation slots in phase e then m_e≤2l_e, so l_e≥ceil(m_e/2). Conversely split the addresses in each phase as evenly as possible between the paths, and allocate exactly ceil(m_e/2) slots there. Pad the shorter path with zero addresses. This uses a common chronological allocation and respects both caps. If the allocation is permanently fixed to l, replace the displayed caps by m_e≤2l_e only.

**Sufficiency, including classical schedule adaptivity.** Fourier-expand the padded addition oracle over the additive group of the response vector. For a fixed history of secret and masks, each complete query path contributes a vector independent of site values, times a character depending only on the compartments on that path. A density-matrix entry expands as a sum over two such paths, and every term is a function only of their union's compartment values. If the criterion holds, the joint distribution of those exact values is secret-independent: each origin appears at t or fewer distinct sites and origin masks are mutually independent. Each term therefore has expectation independent of the secret. For a classically adaptive schedule, first fix a complete schedule-control record, and use unnormalized Kraus branches. Between invocations the corresponding outcome operators are fixed, and within that record both paths have the same actual schedule and obey its caps. Each block of the averaged record-plus-state density operator is thus secret-independent, including its trace. Sum the blocks or keep the classical record as desired. This argument does not condition on an oracle-dependent measurement record and then falsely assert uniform masks; it averages the unnormalized blocks. Quantum processing within any phase may remain arbitrary.

**Necessity, as an abstract oracle distinction.** Suppose a potential pair union covers t+1 distinct evaluations of one origin. Interpolation expresses s as an F-linear functional of those entries, assigning each selected evaluation to one occurrence. Use the balanced path split above. The Fourier labels on the two branches can put their respective terms into the difference of the two characters, with all other coordinates set to zero. The resulting relative phase equals χ(s) for a nontrivial additive character χ of the field and is independent of the sampled polynomial coefficients. An allowed two-path coherence test in this ideal oracle therefore yields different density operators for suitable distinct secrets. More generally choose a nonzero scaling of χ to distinguish any specified distinct field elements (the additive character pairing is nondegenerate). It need not recover a whole scalar; different states already refute perfect privacy. This is a mathematical counterexample in an explicitly hypothetical access model, not a physical query workflow. A single fixed allowed allocation suffices for the counterexample, so the adaptive classical-clock extension has the same universal criterion.

## Why a doubled global count is not the same model

The simpler requirement m_e≤2q_e and Σm_e≤2Q overcounts unions in the chronological invocation model: a lone selected address in each of two phases still requires at least one invocation in each. Example: degree one over an eligible field, two distinct labels of one origin in two singleton phases, q_1=q_2=Q=1. Each ordinary quantum invocation must choose one phase; since it covers only one real address there, with optional zero it supplies no secret information for any state. Yet the naïve doubled global test incorrectly counts both labels and declares nonprivacy.

For a **different**, explicitly stronger experiment, allow a single evaluation of a protected *coherent list interface* with a register L specifying a whole legal chronological list of nonzero addresses (at most q_e per phase, at most Q total). A fixed physical calendar has q_e potential calls in each phase, with unused slots responding as zero; the list register coherently controls which addresses are active in those calls. All potential calls still happen, and no inaccessible device record reveals L or active phase choices. The resource Q in this experiment charges active entries per list, not actual oracle invocations or depth; a list reader may superpose different legal lists. Then the same two-path argument has unions of independently legal lists, without imposing a shared nonzero phase allocation. Those unions are exactly m_e≤2q_e, Σm_e≤2Q: balance each phase, and assign the extra item in each odd-sized phase alternately between lists, so their total sizes differ by at most one. Thus the example is nonprivate in this stronger experiment. This is not a claim about erased hardware clocks, generic coherent adaptive stopping, quantum-accessible production custody, or free controlled calls in the standard query model.

## Exact algorithmic consequence for ordinary fixed-slot clocks

**Width one stays polynomial, though the old flow is no longer exact.** Work on one origin and suppose each compartment gives at most one of its labels. Collapse duplicates inside each phase into an eligibility set of labels. Distinct winning labels may be assigned to the phases where their selected compartments occur; count each label once. This assignment does not increase any slot requirement. A phase with m assigned labels needs ceil(m/2) opened slots, each supplying at most two distinct labels. Conversely any useful slot assignment chooses legitimate singleton compartments. Clip q_e and Q to the number n of distinct labels when larger, and clip Q further to the number S of available slots. This cannot reduce the optimum: every useful opened slot contains at least one distinct chosen label, so there are at most n of them.

Make two clone vertices per phase slot. They have an internal edge to one another (unopened), each has an edge to a distinct private zero-value dummy, and each has edges to every real label eligible in that phase. Solve a maximum-weight matching of **exact cardinality S+Q**. Set M=n+1. Each edge gets M for each clone endpoint plus 1 for each real-label endpoint. The edge to the other clone therefore has weight 2M, a dummy edge M, and a real-label edge M+1. A full clone-saturating cardinality-S+Q matching exists: open any Q slots on dummies and use internal edges in the rest. Every unsaturated matching weighs at most (2S−1)M+n<2SM, strictly less than that saturating candidate. Thus **every optimum saturates both clones of every slot**. A slot then either uses its internal edge or has two external matches, and cardinality forces exactly Q opened slots. A choice using fewer useful slots pads with private dummies. The remaining weight above 2SM equals the number of distinct real labels. Exact-cardinality weighted general matching is polynomial, e.g. add a distinct set of |V|−2(S+Q) new vertices each joined to every original vertex with weight zero and require a maximum-weight perfect matching (no edges among the new vertices). The matching guarantee is an exact computational audit of the stipulated oracle, not an erasure guarantee. For the separate coherent list interface, plain max flow with doubled caps is exact, as in the classical width-one proof.

**Width two makes universal safety coNP-complete for ordinary fixed-slot clocks.** A violating union plus its origin is a polynomial certificate; the rounded constraints can be checked directly. Reduce from perfect 3-dimensional matching with k elements in each role. Give every input triple its own phase with exactly two disjoint paired buffers: the first has that triple's first and second coordinate labels, the second has its third coordinate label and a dummy label unique to the triple. Set q_e=1, Q=k and Shamir degree t=4k−1. Every phase with even one chosen buffer consumes one slot, so reaching 4k labels means opening exactly k phases and taking both buffers in each, with no repeated label. The dummies are unique; the other three labels per phase are the triple coordinates. Thus a certifying union exists iff k triples form a 3-dimensional matching. Choose an eligible field of polynomial size or pad unused coordinates; no numerical shares or oracle circuits are needed in the reduction. The paired buffers are disjoint inside each phase even though opposite phases may repack the same labels. This parallels, but does not reuse, the classical paired-catalog hardness reduction from proper rainbow matching. No complexity theorem claimed for width two in the separate coherent list interface.

## Literature, verification and scope

[Zhandry's limited-independence result](https://doi.org/10.1145/3450745) gives the familiar upper-bound accounting by pairs of q-query paths and at most 2q addresses. [Damgård–Funder–Nielsen–Salvail](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.6313), Theorem 1, already characterize superposed-subset privacy by pair unions. [Childs–van Dam–Hung–Shparlinski](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.09271) and [Zhandry's oracle classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.08352) give much broader exact results on polynomial interpolation/Fourier spectra. Neither halving a Shamir threshold nor the general pair-union principle is presented as new. The narrow consequences here are chronological invocation-clock feasibility with distinct phase rounding, an exact false-positive counterexample to aggregate doubling, and the matching audit/width separation in the copied-buffer setting. The matching and [3D matching](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/BLOGPAPERS/Karp.pdf) primitives themselves are classical. Priority for the restricted setting not established. No coherent real system, hardware protocol, or new cryptographic primitive is claimed.

Pinned Lean 4.34.1 verified the local ceiling split, summed common-slot budget equivalence, explicit odd-phase obstruction, and strict clone-saturation weight priority. Only standard axioms occur. The quantum density argument and graph/complexity reductions remain written proofs, not Lean-formalized end-to-end. Synthetic controls independently enumerated 550 clock/catalog instances and compared phase rounding, coherent path-pair coverage and the exact-cardinality weighted graph gadget; 900 further cases checked the disjoint-pair rank-threshold reduction to 3D matching. No physics, live system, real key material or external model input was tested. Exact AGY tooling not available here; no substitutes or remote changes.
