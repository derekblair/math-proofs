## Finite inputs

* **Marked links:** Aleksandar Bikov, [Small minimal (3,3)-Ramsey graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.03716), Theorem 8.2 and Figure 13. `AtlasEight.lean` checks completeness and the converse using kernel-checked finite cuts, permutations, and blocking colorings.
* **R(3,12)≥53:** The bound is credited to M. Kolodyazhny (2015). The witness in this dossier is the publicly mirrored 52-vertex construction from [Google Research's Ramsey bounds](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/0d94f8c4d92678ce5f7a0eacae725f79174cfb03/ramsey_number_bounds), with its [graph6 mirror](https://github.com/daniel-munro/leaps-in-bounds/tree/main/certificates/ramsey). The bytes of `seed52.g6` hash to `11a6a232da01da935f181a3a4b2238e42fc5ca822bc3300ae3ee9e824b1ec9fd`. The Lean certificate checks the graph as symmetric and loopless, verifies every triangle row, and rules out every independent 12-set. The publicly known bound is not claimed as an improvement.
* **R(3,10)≤41:** Vigleik Angeltveit, [EJC 32(4), P4.30](https://doi.org/10.37236/12936). This is the sole external mathematical input to the final Lean implication beyond the statement's defining hypotheses. The cited computation is not replayed in this bundle.
