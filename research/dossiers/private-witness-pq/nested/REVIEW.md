- Use a field with 32-byte bijective encodings (e.g. GF(2^256)) if claiming arbitrary 256-bit strings and precisely 32-byte additive masks, distinct nonzero public evaluation points at each level; independent fresh outer and inner nonconstant coefficients. No claim of information-theoretic secrecy against 63 raw unmasked shares independently of masks.
- For each potential canonical good family, the real joint law prior to stopping factorizes into its unchanged transcript and the private typed support. The equality is P-weighting and thinning **before averaging**, not equality conditioning on non-disclosure or continued simulation.
