# Public-source priority audit, 25 November 2026

The entries below document checks of the exact propositions, not just title matches. No earlier complete resolution of the stated scopes was located in the public sources checked. This is not an assertion of exhaustive priority: an uncatalogued proof, unpublished manuscript, or unindexed repository could precede the work. Mathematical verification and historical priority are separate questions.
